Chicken Tikka (چکن تکہ)
Pakistan - Mains

Bone-in chicken marinated in yogurt and spices, then grilled or roasted over intense heat until charred outside and juicy within -- a BBQ-night staple served with mint chutney and onion salad.

Key ingredients: bone-in chicken (thigh and leg), yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, red chili powder, garam masala, lemon juice

What it should actually look like: Bone-in chicken pieces (thigh/leg, skin-on) with a deep red-orange charred exterior, distinct blackened grill marks or char spots, served on a platter with onion rings and lemon wedges -- not smooth or saucy like a curry, and not a boneless white-meat cube.

Suggested search terms: "chicken tikka grilled skewers", "Pakistani BBQ chicken tikka", "tandoori chicken tikka charred"
