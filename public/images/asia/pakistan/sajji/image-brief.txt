Sajji (Balochi) (سجی)
Pakistan - Mains

Whole chicken or lamb leg seasoned simply with salt, skewered upright, and slow-roasted over an open fire until deeply golden and smoky, traditionally served with rice cooked in its own drippings -- a Balochi hospitality centerpiece.

Key ingredients: whole chicken or lamb leg, salt, green papaya paste (optional), basmati rice, whole spices

What it should actually look like: A whole roasted chicken or lamb leg, deep golden-brown and smoky-charred on the outside (not covered in a red or dark spice paste like tikka), still mounted on a large skewer or presented whole/carved, served beside a mound of lightly golden rice.

Suggested search terms: "Balochi sajji whole", "Pakistani sajji rice", "Quetta sajji roast"
