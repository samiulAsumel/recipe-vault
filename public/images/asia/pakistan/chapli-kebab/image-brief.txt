Chapli Kebab (چپلی کباب)
Pakistan - Mains

Coarse, wide patties of minced meat mixed with tomato, crushed pomegranate seeds, and spices, shallow-fried until crisp-edged -- a Peshawari specialty named for its flat, sandal-like shape.

Key ingredients: minced beef or mutton, tomato, onion, green chilies, dried pomegranate seeds (anardana), coriander seeds, red chili flakes, cornflour

What it should actually look like: Wide, flat, roughly oval patties (distinctly sandal-shaped, not round like a burger), deeply browned and crisp at the edges, visibly studded with flecks of tomato and coarse spice, served with sliced tomato, onion rings, and lemon.

Suggested search terms: "chapli kebab Peshawar", "Pakistani flat kebab patty", "Peshawari kebab fried"
