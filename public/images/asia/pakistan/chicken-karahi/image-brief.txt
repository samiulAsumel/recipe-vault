Chicken Karahi (چکن کڑاہی)
Pakistan - Mains

Bone-in chicken cooked fast and hot in a wok-like karahi with tomatoes, ginger, garlic, and green chili until the oil visibly separates, finished with a garnish of ginger, coriander, and garam masala -- a defining dish of Pakistani restaurant menus.

Key ingredients: bone-in chicken, tomatoes, ginger-garlic paste, green chilies, red chili powder, coriander seeds, black pepper, ginger julienne, coriander leaves, garam masala

What it should actually look like: Bone-in chicken pieces in a thick, deep-red-orange tomato masala, served directly in the two-handled metal karahi pot it was cooked in, with a visible thin sheen of oil floating on top and fresh ginger/coriander/chili garnish -- not a thin, smooth gravy, and no onions visible in the masala.

Suggested search terms: "chicken karahi", "karahi pot chicken Pakistani", "Lahore chicken karahi restaurant style"
