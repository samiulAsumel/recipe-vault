Sindhi Biryani (سندھی بریانی)
Pakistan - Mains

Layered basmati rice and tender marinated meat, dum-cooked with a tangy tomato masala, dried plums, and green chilies -- a spicier, more sour-forward biryani than most, and a fixture at Pakistani weddings and Eid feasts.

Key ingredients: mutton or chicken, basmati rice, yogurt, tomatoes, dried plums (aloo bukhara), green chilies, biryani masala, fried onions, mint, coriander

What it should actually look like: A mound of long-grain basmati rice with visible orange/yellow color streaks, tender bone-in meat pieces peeking through, dark reddish masala at the base, garnished with fried onions and fresh mint/coriander -- distinctly more reddish-orange and rustic than a smooth, pale Mughlai biryani.
