Kunna Gosht (کُنّہ گوشت)
Pakistan - Mains

Bone-in mutton slow-cooked for hours in a sealed clay pot with minimal water and a fennel- and dried-ginger-heavy spice blend, reducing into an intensely dark, thick gravy -- a Gujranwala specialty named for its cooking vessel.

Key ingredients: bone-in mutton, fennel powder, dried ginger powder, onion, ginger-garlic paste, red chili powder

What it should actually look like: A traditional round clay pot (kunna) with bone-in mutton in an intensely dark brown, thick, almost dry-clinging gravy (much darker and thicker than a typical curry), sometimes photographed still in the clay pot itself rather than a bowl.

Suggested search terms: "kunna gosht clay pot", "Gujranwala kunna", "Pakistani mutton clay pot curry"
