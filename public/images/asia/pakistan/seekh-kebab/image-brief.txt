Seekh Kebab (سیخ کباب)
Pakistan - Mains

Spiced minced beef or mutton molded onto skewers and grilled over charcoal until charred outside and juicy within, served with naan, mint chutney, and a tangy onion salad -- a Pakistani BBQ staple.

Key ingredients: minced beef or mutton, onion, ginger-garlic paste, green chilies, roasted gram flour, cumin, coriander, garam masala, fresh coriander leaves

What it should actually look like: Long cylindrical kebabs still on metal or bamboo skewers, charred and lightly blistered dark brown on the outside, laid on a platter with naan, sliced onion tossed in vinegar, and lemon wedges -- distinctly smooth and sausage-like in shape, not flat or patty-shaped.

Suggested search terms: "seekh kebab skewers", "Pakistani BBQ kebab", "Lahore grilled kebab platter"
