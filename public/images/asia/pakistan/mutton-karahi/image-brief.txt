Mutton Karahi (مٹن کڑاہی)
Pakistan - Mains

Bone-in mutton simmered until tender in a wok-like karahi with tomatoes, ginger, garlic, and green chili, finished with a splash of yogurt and reduced until the oil visibly separates -- a heartier, richer cousin of chicken karahi.

Key ingredients: bone-in mutton, tomatoes, ginger-garlic paste, green chilies, yogurt, red chili powder, coriander seeds, black pepper, ginger julienne, coriander leaves, garam masala

What it should actually look like: Bone-in mutton pieces in a thick, glossy, deep-red-orange masala with visible fat/marrow richness, served in the two-handled metal karahi it was cooked in, with a thin layer of separated oil on top and fresh ginger/coriander/chili garnish -- richer and darker-looking than chicken karahi, not a thin curry.

Suggested search terms: "mutton karahi", "karahi gosht Pakistani", "Lahore mutton karahi restaurant"
