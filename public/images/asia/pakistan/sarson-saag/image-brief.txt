Sarson Saag (سرسوں کا ساگ)
Pakistan - Sides

Mustard greens and spinach slow-cooked until soft, mashed into a thick, coarse purée, and tempered with ghee, onion, and ginger-garlic -- a Punjabi winter staple traditionally eaten with makki di roti.

Key ingredients: mustard greens, spinach, ginger, green chilies, ghee, onion, tomato, corn flour (makki atta)

What it should actually look like: A thick, coarse, deep-green mashed purée in a bowl (not smooth or liquid), with a visible pool of melted golden ghee on top, served alongside a round, pale-yellow makki di roti -- rustic and hand-mashed looking, not blended silky-smooth.

Suggested search terms: "sarson ka saag bowl", "Punjabi mustard greens ghee", "saag makki roti"
