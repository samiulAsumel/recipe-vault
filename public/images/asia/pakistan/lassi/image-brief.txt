Lassi (لسی)
Pakistan - Drinks

Thick, well-set yogurt whisked or blended with water or milk and sugar until smooth and frothy, served chilled -- a Punjabi dairy staple so central to daily life it's sometimes treated as a dish in its own right.

Key ingredients: thick plain yogurt, water or milk, sugar, green cardamom, ice

What it should actually look like: A tall glass filled with a pale, creamy off-white drink topped with a visible layer of froth/foam, condensation on the glass suggesting it's well chilled, optionally garnished with a few dried rose petals or a light dusting of ground cardamom -- not thin or watery looking.

Suggested search terms: "lassi glass frothy", "Punjabi sweet lassi", "Pakistani yogurt drink glass"
