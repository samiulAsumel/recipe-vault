Zarda (زردہ)
Pakistan - Desserts

Basmati rice simmered in a saffron- or yellow-tinted sugar syrup with ghee until tender, glossy, and golden, topped with almonds, pistachios, and raisins -- a festive sweet rice served at Pakistani weddings and Eid.

Key ingredients: basmati rice, sugar, ghee, saffron (or yellow food color), almonds, pistachios, raisins

What it should actually look like: A shallow dish or platter of glossy, bright golden-yellow rice grains (separate, not clumped or mushy), generously topped with sliced almonds, chopped pistachios, and plump raisins, sometimes with a few sheets of edible silver leaf -- distinctly a sweet, celebratory rice dish, not a savory pulao.

Suggested search terms: "zarda rice sweet", "Pakistani wedding zarda", "saffron rice dessert Pakistan"
