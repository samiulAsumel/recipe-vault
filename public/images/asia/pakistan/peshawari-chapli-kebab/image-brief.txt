Peshawari Chapli Kebab (پشاوری چپلی کباب)
Pakistan - Mains

Coarse minced meat enriched with tail fat and whole coriander seeds, mixed with tomato and pomegranate seeds, shaped into wide patties and shallow-fried until deeply crisp -- Peshawar's own benchmark version of chapli kebab.

Key ingredients: minced beef or mutton, beef or lamb tail fat, tomato, whole coriander seeds, dried pomegranate seeds (anardana), red chili flakes

What it should actually look like: Wide, flat, sandal-shaped patties, deeply browned and crisp at the edges, visibly glistening with rendered fat, studded with whole coriander seeds and flecks of tomato -- richer and glossier-looking than a standard chapli kebab.

Suggested search terms: "Peshawari chapli kebab", "Peshawar kebab crispy", "Pakistani chapli kebab fried"
