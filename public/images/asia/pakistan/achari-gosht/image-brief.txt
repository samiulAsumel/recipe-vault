Achari Gosht (اچاری گوشت)
Pakistan - Mains

Bone-in mutton simmered in a masala built on whole pickling spices -- mustard seed, fennel, nigella, and fenugreek -- with mustard oil and a splash of vinegar, giving it a distinctly tangy, pickle-like character.

Key ingredients: bone-in mutton, mustard seeds, fennel seeds, nigella seeds, fenugreek seeds, mustard oil, vinegar, tomato, yogurt

What it should actually look like: A mutton curry in a thick, glossy, reddish-brown sauce with clearly visible whole spices (mustard seeds, fennel, dried red chilies) scattered throughout -- distinctly speckled and rustic-looking rather than a smooth, uniform gravy.

Suggested search terms: "achari gosht curry", "Pakistani pickle spiced mutton", "achari mutton whole spices"
