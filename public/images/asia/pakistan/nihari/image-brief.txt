Nihari (نہاری)
Pakistan - Mains

Bone-in beef shank slow-simmered for hours into a deep reddish-brown, richly spiced stew with tender marrow, thickened with wheat flour and finished with a sizzling ghee-and-chili tarka -- a Lahore and Karachi institution.

Key ingredients: beef shank, ghee, onion, ginger-garlic paste, nihari masala, red chili powder, wheat flour, ginger, green chilies, coriander leaves, lemon

What it should actually look like: A deep reddish-brown stew in a bowl, glossy on top with a thin sheen of red-tinted oil (the tarka), topped with julienned ginger, sliced green chili, and coriander leaves, with a lemon wedge on the side -- thinner and glossier than a thick curry, not a dry roast.

Suggested search terms: "Pakistani nihari", "beef nihari bowl", "Lahore nihari breakfast"
