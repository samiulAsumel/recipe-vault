Haleem (حلیم)
Pakistan - Mains

Wheat, barley, and lentils slow-cooked with bone-in meat until everything breaks down into a thick, smooth, porridge-like stew, finished with a sizzling cumin-ghee tarka and a generous garnish -- a Ramadan and Eid Milad-un-Nabi staple, especially in Karachi.

Key ingredients: broken wheat, pearl barley, chana dal, masoor dal, moong dal, beef or mutton, ghee, cumin seeds, fried onions, ginger, green chilies, coriander, mint

What it should actually look like: A thick, smooth, uniform brownish-beige paste in a bowl, with no visible whole grains or meat strands, topped with a pool of golden ghee, crispy fried onions, julienned ginger, green chili slices, and chopped herbs -- closer to a thick porridge than a chunky stew.

Suggested search terms: "Pakistani haleem bowl", "Karachi haleem Ramadan", "beef haleem garnished"
