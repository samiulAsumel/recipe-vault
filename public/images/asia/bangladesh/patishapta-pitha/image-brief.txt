Patishapta Pitha (পাটিসাপটা পিঠা)
Bangladesh - Desserts

Thin rice-flour crepes rolled around a rich, milk-reduced coconut and date-palm jaggery filling — a beloved winter pitha, closer to a delicate dessert crepe than a bread.

Key ingredients: whole milk, date-palm jaggery (khejur gur), grated, fresh coconut, grated, rice flour, all-purpose flour (maida), semolina (suji), milk, sugar, salt, ghee or oil

What it should actually look like: Thin rolled rice-flour crepe(s) with a soft coconut-jaggery kheer filling visible at the cut ends - not a Western dessert crepe with chocolate.
