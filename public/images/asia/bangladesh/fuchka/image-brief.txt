Fuchka (ফুচকা)
Bangladesh - Snacks

Small, hollow, crisp-fried puris cracked open, filled with mashed potato and chickpeas, and flooded with tangy tamarind-mint water — one of Bangladesh's most beloved street snacks, eaten fresh one piece at a time.

Key ingredients: semolina (suji), all-purpose flour, water, salt, oil, potatoes, boiled and mashed coarsely, chickpeas, boiled, onion, finely chopped, salt, tamarind pulp, mint leaves, coriander leaves, green chilies, roasted cumin powder, black salt, sugar, cold water

What it should actually look like: Small round hollow crisp puris, cracked open showing potato-chickpea filling, often served with a small bowl of brownish-green tamarind water for pouring in.
