Kala Bhuna (কালা ভুনা)
Bangladesh - Mains

Beef reduced for hours in mustard oil and whole spices until the sauce turns nearly black and clings to the meat like a thick glaze — Chittagong's most celebrated slow-cooked curry.

Key ingredients: beef, bone-in curry-cut, ginger-garlic paste, red chili powder, turmeric powder, salt, mustard oil, onion, very thinly sliced, whole spices (bay leaf, cinnamon, cardamom, cloves, black cardamom), cumin-coriander powder, hot water

What it should actually look like: Bone-in beef in a nearly black, glossy, thick reduced sauce - darker than regular bhuna.
