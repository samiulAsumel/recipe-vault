Ilish Bhaja (ইলিশ ভাজা)
Bangladesh - Sides

Hilsa steaks rubbed with turmeric and salt, then shallow-fried in mustard oil until crisp-edged — the simplest and most everyday way hilsa is eaten.

Key ingredients: hilsa fish, cut into steaks, turmeric powder, red chili powder, salt, mustard oil

What it should actually look like: Round cross-section hilsa steaks (not long fillets) - shows the spine ring - deep golden-brown, crisp-edged, pan-fried.
