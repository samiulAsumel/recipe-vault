Haleem (Dhaka style) (হালিম)
Bangladesh - Mains

A thick, slow-simmered porridge of wheat, barley, and lentils pounded together with shredded beef, finished with fried onion, ginger, and lemon — Dhaka's essential Ramadan iftar dish.

Key ingredients: cracked wheat (dalia), pearl barley, mixed lentils (masoor, moong, chana dal), water, beef, bone-in curry-cut, ginger-garlic paste, turmeric powder, red chili powder, whole spices (bay leaf, cinnamon, cardamom, cloves), ghee, onion, sliced, salt, fried onion (beresta), ginger, julienned, green chilies, sliced, coriander leaves, chopped, lemon, cut into wedges, chaat masala

What it should actually look like: Thick, unified porridge-like consistency (no distinct grains or meat strands visible), brown/tan color, generously topped with fried onion, ginger julienne, green chili, coriander, lemon.
