Begun Bhaja (বেগুন ভাজা)
Bangladesh - Sides

Eggplant slices rubbed with turmeric and chili, then shallow-fried until golden and soft — a simple, everyday Bengali side dish.

Key ingredients: eggplant, sliced into rounds, salt, turmeric powder, red chili powder, mustard oil

What it should actually look like: Amber golden-brown fried eggplant rounds, crisp edges, often flecked with nigella seeds.
