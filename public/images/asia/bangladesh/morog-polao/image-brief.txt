Morog Polao (মোরগ পোলাও)
Bangladesh - Mains

Bone-in chicken pieces slow-cooked with fragrant basmati rice, whole spices, and caramelized onions in a single pot — a lighter, everyday cousin of kacchi biryani.

Key ingredients: chicken, bone-in pieces, plain yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, white pepper powder, salt, basmati rice, ghee, whole spices (bay leaf, cinnamon, cardamom, cloves), onion, sliced, warm milk with saffron, fried cashews and raisins, green chilies, chicken stock or hot water

What it should actually look like: Creamy whitish rice (not deep yellow), bone-in chicken pieces, golden fried onion, cashews/raisins on top.
