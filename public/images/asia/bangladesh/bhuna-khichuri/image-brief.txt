Bhuna Khichuri (ভুনা খিচুড়ি)
Bangladesh - Mains

Rice and lentils slow-fried in ghee and whole spices before simmering into a rich, fragrant one-pot khichuri — Bangladesh's definitive rainy-day comfort food.

Key ingredients: rice (short-grain or chinigura), moong dal, split, ghee, onion, sliced, ginger-garlic paste, turmeric powder, cumin powder, whole spices (bay leaf, cinnamon, cardamom), dried red chili, salt, hot water, green chilies, potato, cubed, cauliflower florets

What it should actually look like: Dry, grainy, non-mushy texture (jhurjhure), yellowish-golden rice+lentils, ghee drizzle.
