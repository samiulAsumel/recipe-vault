Bakarkhani (বাকরখানি)
Bangladesh - Breads

A thick, flaky, faintly sweet layered flatbread with a crisp, golden crust and nigella-seed topping — an Old Dhaka bread with Mughal-era roots, traditionally torn and dipped in tea.

Key ingredients: all-purpose flour (maida), milk, lukewarm, sugar, yeast, salt, ghee, melted, water, ghee, softened, nigella seeds (kalonji), milk

What it should actually look like: Thick, round, flaky layered bread with a golden-brown crust and visible flaky layers at the edge, nigella seeds on top - NOT a thin roti or naan.

Suggested search terms: "Bakarkhani", "Bakarkhani recipe", "বাকরখানি"
