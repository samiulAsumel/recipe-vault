Piyaju (পিয়াজু)
Bangladesh - Snacks

Split lentils ground into a coarse batter with onion, chili, and coriander, deep-fried into small golden fritters — an essential Ramadan iftar item, sold by the plateful across Bangladesh every evening during the fasting month.

Key ingredients: split lentils (masoor dal or a mix), soaked at least 2 hours, onion, finely chopped, green chilies, finely chopped, coriander leaves, chopped, ginger, grated, cumin powder, salt, oil

What it should actually look like: Small, round-to-irregular golden-brown deep-fried lentil fritters, coarse/rustic surface texture (not smooth like a pakora), usually stacked on a plate.
