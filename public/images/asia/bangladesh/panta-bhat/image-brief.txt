Panta Bhat (পান্তা ভাত)
Bangladesh - Mains

Rice soaked overnight in water until lightly fermented and tangy, served cold with green chili, onion, and a squeeze of lime — a rural breakfast turned Pohela Boishakh icon.

Key ingredients: cooked rice, water, salt, onion, finely chopped, green chili, finely chopped, mustard oil, lime, cut into wedges, dried fish or fried hilsa (shutki or ilish bhaja)

What it should actually look like: Rice is the main subject, submerged/soaked look, served with sliced onion, green chili, and a piece of fried fish (not a whole raw fish) and lime.
