Mangsher Jhol (মাংসের ঝোল)
Bangladesh - Mains

Bone-in beef or mutton and potato simmered in a thin, lightly spiced turmeric broth — the everyday, brothy counterpart to Bengal's richer, slow-reduced meat curries.

Key ingredients: beef or mutton, bone-in curry-cut, ginger-garlic paste, turmeric powder, salt, mustard oil or regular cooking oil, onion, thinly sliced, cumin-coriander powder, red chili powder, potatoes, halved, hot water, coriander leaves, chopped

What it should actually look like: Bone-in meat and potato in a thin, turmeric-gold, brothy gravy (light and soupy, not thick) - similar in style to machher jhol but with meat.
