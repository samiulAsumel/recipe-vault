Sandesh (সন্দেশ)
Bangladesh - Desserts

Fresh chhena kneaded with sugar and gently cooked just enough to bind, pressed into decorative molded shapes — Bengal's "dry" chhena sweet, with no syrup, letting the cheese's delicate flavor come through.

Key ingredients: whole milk, lemon juice or vinegar, sugar (fine or powdered), green cardamom powder

What it should actually look like: Pale cream/off-white molded sweets with decorative shapes (flowers, shells) pressed in, dry (not wet/glossy like rasgulla), small bite-sized pieces.
