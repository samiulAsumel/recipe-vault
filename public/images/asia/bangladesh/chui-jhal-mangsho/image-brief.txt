Chui Jhal Mangsho (চুইঝাল মাংস)
Bangladesh - Mains

Beef or mutton simmered with sliced chui jhal stem — a peppery, aromatic regional spice that gives this southwestern Bangladeshi curry its distinctive warming heat.

Key ingredients: beef or mutton, bone-in curry-cut, ginger-garlic paste, red chili powder, turmeric powder, salt, mustard oil, onion, thinly sliced, chui jhal stem, peeled and thinly sliced, cumin-coriander powder, green chilies, hot water

What it should actually look like: Beef/mutton curry with visible thin pale-green slices of chui (Piper chaba) stem mixed through a brown gravy.
