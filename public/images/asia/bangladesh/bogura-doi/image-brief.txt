Bogura Doi (বগুড়ার দই)
Bangladesh - Desserts

Thick, caramel-colored sweetened yogurt set in clay pots, its light caramel flavor coming from milk slowly reduced and lightly caramelized before setting — Bogura district's signature sweet.

Key ingredients: whole milk, sugar, plain yogurt

What it should actually look like: Thick, caramel/light-brown colored set yogurt, usually shown in a small clay pot, smooth glossy top surface.
