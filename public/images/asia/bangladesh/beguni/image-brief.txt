Beguni (বেগুনি)
Bangladesh - Snacks

Thin eggplant slices dipped in a spiced gram-flour batter and deep-fried until crisp — alongside piyaju, one of the two most essential items on a Bangladeshi Ramadan iftar table.

Key ingredients: eggplant, sliced thin into rounds, salt, gram flour (besan), rice flour, turmeric powder, red chili powder, nigella seeds (kalonji), salt, water, oil

What it should actually look like: Thin round eggplant slices coated in a golden-yellow gram-flour batter, deep-fried until crisp - flat disc shape, batter should look bumpy/textured not smooth.
