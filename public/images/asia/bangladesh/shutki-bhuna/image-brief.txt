Shutki Bhuna (শুটকি ভুনা)
Bangladesh - Sides

Sun-dried fish rehydrated and dry-fried with onion, garlic, and a heavy hand of chili — an intensely pungent, deeply savory accompaniment central to Bangladesh's coastal and riverine cooking.

Key ingredients: dried fish (shutki), cleaned and soaked, turmeric powder, mustard oil, onion, thinly sliced, garlic, sliced, ginger, julienned, green chilies, red chili powder, salt

What it should actually look like: Dry-fried shredded dried fish with browned onion/garlic/chili, dry texture, no pooling sauce.
