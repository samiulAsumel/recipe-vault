Chomchom (চমচম)
Bangladesh - Desserts

Oval-shaped chhena sweets boiled in sugar syrup, then coated in a soft khoya crumble or dry sugar glaze — closely associated with Tangail's Porabari area, famous enough to function as its own regional sweet brand.

Key ingredients: whole milk, lemon juice or vinegar, all-purpose flour or semolina, sugar, water, milk, sugar

What it should actually look like: Oval/elongated white or cream-colored sweets, either coated in a grainy khoya crumble topping or rolled in dry white sugar - distinctly oval, not round like rasgulla.
