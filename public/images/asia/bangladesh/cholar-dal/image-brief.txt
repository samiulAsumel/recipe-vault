Cholar Dal (ছোলার ডাল)
Bangladesh - Sides

Bengal gram lentils simmered with coconut, raisins, and a touch of sugar into a slightly sweet, richly spiced dal — a festive-meal staple, especially alongside luchi.

Key ingredients: chana dal (split Bengal gram), soaked, turmeric powder, water, salt, sugar, ghee, whole spices (bay leaf, cinnamon, cardamom), dried red chili, cumin powder, coconut, thinly sliced, raisins

What it should actually look like: Glossy yellow Bengal gram dal topped with golden fried coconut slivers and dark raisins, often served with luchi.
