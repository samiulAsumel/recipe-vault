Cha (চা)
Bangladesh - Drinks

Black tea boiled with milk and sugar until strong and richly colored — the drink around which Bangladesh's entire adda (unhurried conversation) culture is built, sold from countless roadside tea stalls.

Key ingredients: water, black tea leaves (CTC-style), whole milk, sugar, green cardamom, lightly crushed

What it should actually look like: A cup or small glass of reddish-brown milk tea, steaming hot, sometimes with a thin froth on top - NOT black coffee-dark, more medium brown from milk.
