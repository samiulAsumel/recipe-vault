Shukto (শুক্তো)
Bangladesh - Soups

A mixed-vegetable stew built around a gentle bitter note from bitter gourd, traditionally eaten as the opening course of a formal Bengali meal.

Key ingredients: bitter gourd, sliced, potato, cubed, eggplant, cubed, drumstick (moringa), cut into pieces, raw plantain, cubed, ghee, panch phoron (five-spice blend), ginger paste, mustard paste, milk, water, salt, sugar

What it should actually look like: Pale, mild, milk-based mixed vegetable stew (bitter gourd, potato, eggplant, drumstick) - NOT red/orange, should look creamy/pale.
