Kacchi Biryani (কাচ্চি বিরিয়ানি)
Bangladesh - Mains

Mutton and potatoes dum-cooked with fragrant basmati rice, sealed and steamed together rather than layered after separate cooking.

Key ingredients: mutton, bone-in curry-cut, plain yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, red chili powder, salt, basmati rice, whole spices (bay leaf, cinnamon, cardamom), salt, fried onion (beresta), saffron, potatoes, ghee, kewra water

What it should actually look like: Soft brownish-gold rice (not multicolored), tender mutton pieces, fried onion + potato visible, served in a clay handi.
