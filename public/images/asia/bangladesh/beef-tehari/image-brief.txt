Beef Tehari (গরুর তেহারি)
Bangladesh - Mains

Bone-in beef and potatoes simmered together with mustard-oil-tinted rice in a single pot — Dhaka's everyday, more affordable cousin of kacchi biryani.

Key ingredients: beef, bone-in curry-cut, plain yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, red chili powder, turmeric powder, salt, mustard oil, onion, thinly sliced, whole spices (bay leaf, cinnamon, cardamom, cloves), basmati rice, potatoes, green chilies, ghee, salt, fried onion (beresta), coriander leaves

What it should actually look like: Light brown toasted rice, bone-in beef chunks, whole green chilies mixed through, mustard-oil sheen.
