Chicken Rezala (চিকেন রেজালা)
Bangladesh - Mains

Bone-in chicken simmered in a pale, creamy yogurt-cashew gravy seasoned with white pepper rather than red chili, kept deliberately light in color — a mild, refined Mughlai-heritage curry served at Bangladeshi weddings.

Key ingredients: chicken, bone-in pieces, plain yogurt, whisked smooth, ginger-garlic paste, white pepper powder, salt, ghee, onion, thinly sliced, whole spices (green cardamom, cinnamon, bay leaf), cashew paste, green chilies, slit, sugar, hot water, garam masala powder

What it should actually look like: Pale/white creamy gravy (NOT golden-brown or red) - deliberately light-colored, chicken pieces in a smooth, mild-looking sauce.
