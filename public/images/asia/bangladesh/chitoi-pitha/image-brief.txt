Chitoi Pitha (চিতই পিঠা)
Bangladesh - Breads

A thick, softly steamed rice-flour cake with a lightly golden crust on the bottom — plain on its own, but built to be eaten with date-palm jaggery, yogurt, or a rich duck curry.

Key ingredients: rice flour, water, salt, oil, date-palm jaggery (khejur gur), plain yogurt (doi), duck curry (hashher mangsho) or other rich meat curry

What it should actually look like: Thick round steamed rice cake, golden-brown crusty bottom, soft porous white top, often served with molasses or duck curry.
