Rasgulla (Roshogolla) (রসগোল্লা (রসগোল্লা))
Bangladesh - Desserts

Soft, spongy balls of fresh cheese boiled in a light sugar syrup until they swell and turn airy — one of Bengal's most iconic sweets, equally beloved in Bangladesh as a festive and everyday dessert.

Key ingredients: whole milk, lemon juice or vinegar, all-purpose flour or semolina, sugar, water, cardamom pods, lightly crushed

What it should actually look like: White, round, spongy balls sitting in a clear light sugar syrup, usually in a bowl - NOT golden or brown, should look pale white and glossy wet.
