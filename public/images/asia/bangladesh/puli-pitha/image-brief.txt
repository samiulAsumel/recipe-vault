Puli Pitha (পুলি পিঠা)
Bangladesh - Desserts

Half-moon rice-flour dumplings filled with sweet coconut and date-palm jaggery, sealed at the edge and boiled or steamed until tender — a classic shape in Bengal's winter pitha repertoire.

Key ingredients: fresh coconut, grated, date-palm jaggery (khejur gur), grated, green cardamom, crushed, rice flour, water, salt

What it should actually look like: Half-moon sealed dumplings (not flat discs), pale white/cream dough, may show coconut-jaggery filling if cut open.
