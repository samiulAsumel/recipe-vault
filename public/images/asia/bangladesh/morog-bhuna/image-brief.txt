Morog Bhuna (মোরগ ভুনা)
Bangladesh - Mains

Bone-in chicken dry-fried in a thick, spiced onion masala until the sauce clings fully to the meat — a quicker, everyday chicken counterpart to beef bhuna.

Key ingredients: chicken, bone-in pieces, plain yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, red chili powder, turmeric powder, salt, mustard oil, onion, thinly sliced, whole spices (bay leaf, cinnamon, cardamom), cumin-coriander powder, green chilies, slit, hot water, coriander leaves, chopped

What it should actually look like: Chicken pieces in a thick, dark reduced masala clinging to the meat, similar to beef bhuna but with chicken - minimal standing sauce.
