Beef Bhuna (বীফ ভুনা)
Bangladesh - Mains

Beef dry-fried down in a spiced onion masala until thick and richly coated — a everyday staple across Bangladeshi households.

Key ingredients: beef, bone-in curry-cut, plain yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, red chili powder, turmeric powder, salt, mustard oil, onion, thinly sliced, whole spices (bay leaf, cinnamon, cardamom), cumin-coriander powder, green chilies, hot water

What it should actually look like: Bone-in beef in a thick, dark, unctuous reduced masala that clings to the meat, minimal pooled liquid.
