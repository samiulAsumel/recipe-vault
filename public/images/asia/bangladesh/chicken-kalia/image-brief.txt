Chicken Kalia (চিকেন কালিয়া)
Bangladesh - Mains

Bone-in chicken and potato simmered in a rich, golden-brown onion-yogurt gravy generously spiced with whole garam masala — a saucier, Mughlai-heritage curry meant to be eaten with plenty of rice.

Key ingredients: chicken, bone-in pieces, plain yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, turmeric powder, salt, potatoes, halved, ghee or oil, onion, sliced, whole spices (bay leaf, cinnamon, cardamom, cloves), red chili powder, sugar, hot water, garam masala powder

What it should actually look like: Chicken and potato in a rich golden-brown gravy with a generous pool of sauce (wetter than bhuna, not dry-clinging).
