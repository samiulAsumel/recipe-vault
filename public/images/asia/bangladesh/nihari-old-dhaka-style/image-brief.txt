Nihari (Old Dhaka style) (নেহারি)
Bangladesh - Mains

Bone-in beef shank slow-simmered for hours into a deep reddish-brown, richly spiced stew with tender marrow, thickened with wheat flour and finished with ginger, chili, and lemon — an Old Dhaka breakfast institution.

Key ingredients: beef shank, bone-in, with marrow, ghee or oil, onion, sliced, ginger-garlic paste, nihari masala (fennel, dried ginger, whole spices, ground), red chili powder, salt, water, wheat flour (atta), ginger, julienned, green chilies, sliced, coriander leaves, chopped, lemon, cut into wedges

What it should actually look like: Deep reddish-brown, glossy, moderately thick stew with visible bone-in shank pieces (often with marrow bone standing up), garnished with ginger julienne and coriander.
