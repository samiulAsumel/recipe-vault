Chingri Bhapa (চিংড়ি ভাপা)
Bangladesh - Mains

Prawns steamed in a mustard-coconut paste inside a covered bowl set over simmering water — a gentler, all-in-one-vessel cousin of paturi, without the banana-leaf wrapping.

Key ingredients: mustard seeds (yellow), soaked, grated coconut, green chilies, mustard oil, turmeric powder, salt, large prawns, peeled and deveined, turmeric powder, salt, green chilies, mustard oil

What it should actually look like: Prawns (pink with red tails) coated in mustard-coconut-yogurt paste, steamed.
