Hilsa Paturi (ইলিশ পাতুরি)
Bangladesh - Mains

Hilsa fillets coated in a pungent mustard-coconut paste, wrapped in banana leaf, and steamed until the leaf itself perfumes the fish.

Key ingredients: mustard seeds (yellow and black), soaked, green chilies, grated coconut, mustard oil, turmeric powder, salt, hilsa fish, cut into steaks, mustard oil, banana leaf, cut into 4 large pieces, kitchen string or toothpicks

What it should actually look like: Fish coated in vivid green mustard-coconut paste, wrapped and steamed in banana leaf (leaf edges dark/charred when unwrapped).
