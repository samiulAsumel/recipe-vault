Jhalmuri (ঝালমুড়ি)
Bangladesh - Snacks

Puffed rice tossed with mustard oil, chopped onion, tomato, green chili, peanuts, and crunchy chanachur mix — a no-cook Bengali street snack mixed to order in a folded newspaper cone.

Key ingredients: puffed rice (muri), chanachur (crunchy fried lentil-noodle mix), onion, finely chopped, tomato, finely chopped, green chilies, finely chopped, roasted peanuts, mustard oil, chaat masala, salt, lime, cut into wedges, coriander leaves, chopped

What it should actually look like: Puffed rice mixed with visible chopped tomato, onion, peanuts, and crunchy chanachur bits, often served in a paper cone - a dry mix, NOT a wet curry.
