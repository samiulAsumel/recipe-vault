Mishti Doi (মিষ্টি দই)
Bangladesh - Desserts

Milk sweetened and lightly caramelized before being set into a firm, custard-like sweet yogurt — the foundational Bengali dessert from which regional specialties like Bogura doi have developed their own techniques.

Key ingredients: whole milk, sugar, plain yogurt

What it should actually look like: Set yogurt with a light caramel/tan color, smooth glossy top, usually in a small clay pot or bowl - firmer and more custard-like than plain yogurt.
