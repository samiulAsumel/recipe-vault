Borhani (বোরহানি)
Bangladesh - Drinks

A tangy, spiced yogurt drink blended with mustard, mint, roasted cumin, and black salt — the essential companion to Bangladesh's richest festive dishes, especially kacchi biryani, cutting through the richness of a heavy meal.

Key ingredients: plain yogurt, whisked smooth, cold water, mustard paste, mint leaves, coriander leaves, green chilies, roasted cumin powder, black salt, salt, sugar

What it should actually look like: A pale green-tinged or off-white smooth yogurt drink in a glass, NOT thick/orange like a mango lassi - flecked possibly with tiny herb bits before straining.

Suggested search terms: "Borhani", "Borhani recipe", "বোরহানি"
