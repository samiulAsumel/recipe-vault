Shorshe Ilish (সরষে ইলিশ)
Bangladesh - Mains

Hilsa steaks simmered in a pungent raw mustard-seed and mustard-oil gravy — arguably the single most iconic Bengali fish curry.

Key ingredients: yellow mustard seeds, soaked, green chilies, mustard oil, turmeric powder, salt, hilsa fish, cut into steaks, mustard oil, turmeric powder, nigella seeds (kalojeera), water, green chilies

What it should actually look like: Hilsa steaks in a thick, textured golden-yellow mustard paste flecked with black mustard seeds (paste clings to fish, not a thin gravy).
