Porota (পরোটা)
Bangladesh - Breads

A layered, flaky flatbread folded and coiled before rolling and pan-frying in ghee or oil — Bangladesh's everyday breakfast bread, eaten daily with egg, dal, or bhaji.

Key ingredients: whole wheat flour (atta), water, salt, oil, ghee or oil, dry flour

What it should actually look like: Layered, flaky flatbread, golden-brown with visible separated layers/flakes, usually round or square-ish, pan-fried (not deep fried).
