Ilish Bhorta (ইলিশ ভর্তা)
Bangladesh - Sides

Fried hilsa fish, deboned and mashed with mustard oil, raw onion, and green chili into an intensely flavored bhorta — a way to enjoy hilsa's rich flavor without its famously fine bones.

Key ingredients: hilsa fish, cut into steaks, turmeric powder, salt, mustard oil, onion, finely chopped, green chilies, finely chopped, mustard oil, salt, coriander leaves, chopped

What it should actually look like: Mashed/flaked fish mixed with visible onion and chili bits, mounded on a plate, NOT whole fish pieces - a coarse mash texture.
