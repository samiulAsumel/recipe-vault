Aloo Bhorta (আলু ভর্তা)
Bangladesh - Sides

Boiled potatoes mashed by hand with mustard oil, raw onion, and green chili — the single most common side dish on a Bangladeshi dinner table.

Key ingredients: potatoes, onion, finely chopped, green chilies, finely chopped, mustard oil, salt, coriander leaves, chopped

What it should actually look like: Mashed potato mixed with raw onion, green chili, mustard oil - mounded, glossy, not smooth/pureed.
