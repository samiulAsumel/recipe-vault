Kheer (ক্ষীর)
Bangladesh - Desserts

Rice slow-simmered in milk until thick and creamy, sweetened and scented with cardamom, finished with nuts and raisins — one of South Asia's most widespread desserts, central to Bangladeshi festival and celebration cooking.

Key ingredients: whole milk, rice (short-grain or basmati), rinsed and soaked briefly, sugar, green cardamom powder, ghee, cashews, halved, raisins, almonds, slivered

What it should actually look like: Thick, creamy white/off-white rice pudding in a bowl, studded with golden fried cashews, raisins, and almond slivers on top.
