Machher Jhol (মাছের ঝোল)
Bangladesh - Mains

A light, turmeric-gold fish curry in a thin, well-spiced broth — the everyday backbone of a Bengali rice meal, cooked with fresh river or pond fish.

Key ingredients: rui (rohu) or katla (catla) fish, cut into steaks, turmeric powder, salt, mustard oil, mustard oil, nigella seeds (kalonji), onion, thinly sliced, ginger-garlic paste, tomato, chopped, turmeric powder, cumin-coriander powder, green chilies, slit, water, salt, coriander leaves, chopped

What it should actually look like: Bone-in river fish + potato in a thin, warm turmeric-tomato broth (light, brothy, not thick).
