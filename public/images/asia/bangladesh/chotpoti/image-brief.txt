Chotpoti (চটপটি)
Bangladesh - Snacks

White peas and potato simmered in a tangy, tamarind-spiked stew, topped with chopped onion, boiled egg, and tetul sauce — an iconic Dhaka street snack sold from carts across the city.

Key ingredients: dried white peas, soaked overnight, potato, cubed, turmeric powder, cumin-coriander powder, salt, water, tamarind pulp, sugar, red chili powder, boiled eggs, halved, onion, finely chopped, green chilies, finely chopped, coriander leaves, chopped

What it should actually look like: A bowl of stewed white peas/potato in a brownish tangy gravy, topped with sliced boiled egg, chopped raw onion, green chili, and a dark tamarind sauce drizzle.
