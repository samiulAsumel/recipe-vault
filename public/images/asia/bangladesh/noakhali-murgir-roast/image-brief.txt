Noakhali Murgir Roast (নোয়াখালীর মুরগীর রোস্ট)
Bangladesh - Mains

Bone-in chicken dry-reduced with caramelized sugar and warm spices into a dark, glossy, sweet-savory glaze with almost no standing sauce — a Noakhali regional wedding-feast specialty.

Key ingredients: chicken, bone-in pieces, plain yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, red chili powder, salt, sugar, ghee or oil, onion, fried (beresta), whole spices (bay leaf, cinnamon, cardamom, cloves), cashew-raisin paste, garam masala powder, hot water, kewra water

What it should actually look like: Dark reddish-brown, glossy, thick glaze on chicken pieces (very little standing sauce), similar look to kala bhuna but with chicken.
