Bhapa Pitha (ভাপা পিঠা)
Bangladesh - Desserts

A steamed cylinder of soft rice flour packed with sweet grated coconut and date-palm jaggery — a beloved winter breakfast pitha, best eaten hot straight from the steamer.

Key ingredients: rice flour (chal gura), warm water, salt, fresh coconut, grated, date-palm jaggery (khejur gur), grated

What it should actually look like: Firm, slightly translucent white steamed cylindrical rice cake, cut open shows a coconut + date-palm-jaggery filling.
