Chingri Malaikari (চিংড়ি মালাইকারি)
Bangladesh - Mains

Large prawns simmered in a rich, lightly spiced coconut milk gravy — a festive Bengali curry traditionally served at weddings and special celebrations.

Key ingredients: large prawns (tiger prawns), peeled and deveined, turmeric powder, salt, onion paste, ginger-garlic paste, coconut milk, thin, coconut milk, thick, bay leaf, cinnamon stick, green cardamom, cumin powder, red chili powder, green chilies, sugar, mustard oil or ghee, salt

What it should actually look like: Large prawns in a rich golden-orange creamy coconut milk gravy.
