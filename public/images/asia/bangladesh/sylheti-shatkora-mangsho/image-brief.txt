Sylheti Shatkora Mangsho (সিলেটি সাতকরা মাংস)
Bangladesh - Mains

Bone-in beef simmered with shatkora, a large bitter-aromatic citrus unique to the Sylhet region, giving the curry a distinctive tangy-bitter fragrance found nowhere else in Bengali cooking.

Key ingredients: beef, bone-in curry-cut, ginger-garlic paste, turmeric powder, red chili powder, salt, shatkora, mustard oil, onion, thinly sliced, cumin-coriander powder, green chilies, slit, hot water

What it should actually look like: Beef curry with visible pale-green/yellowish citrus rind pieces (shatkora) mixed through a brown gravy - the citrus pieces look different from onion or other vegetables.
