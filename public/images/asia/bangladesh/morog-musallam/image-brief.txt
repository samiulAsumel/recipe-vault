Morog Musallam (মোরগ মোসাল্লাম)
Bangladesh - Mains

A whole chicken marinated in yogurt and spices, then slow-braised in a rich cashew-thickened gravy and served intact, garnished with boiled eggs and fried onion — a Mughlai-heritage festive centerpiece.

Key ingredients: whole chicken, plain yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, red chili powder, turmeric powder, salt, ghee, onion, sliced, cashew paste, whole spices (bay leaf, cinnamon, cardamom, cloves), garam masala powder, hot water, boiled eggs, fried onion (beresta)

What it should actually look like: A WHOLE chicken (not pieces) on a platter, glossy gravy, garnished with boiled eggs and fried onion.
