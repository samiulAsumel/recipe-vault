Aam Pora Sherbot (আম পোড়া শরবত)
Bangladesh - Drinks

Roasted or charred green mango pulp blended with sugar, black salt, and roasted cumin into a smoky, sweet-tangy drink — one of Bangladesh's defining summer coolers, traditionally tied to Pohela Boishakh celebrations.

Key ingredients: raw green mango, sugar, black salt, roasted cumin powder, salt, cold water, mint leaves

What it should actually look like: A pale gold/tan smooth drink in a glass with ice, garnished with mint - NOT bright yellow/orange like ripe mango juice, since it's made from roasted green mango.
