Kosha Mangsho (কষা মাংস)
Bangladesh - Mains

Mutton slow-cooked and dry-reduced in a deeply spiced onion-yogurt masala until the sauce clings thickly to the meat — Bengal's definitive Sunday lunch curry.

Key ingredients: mutton, bone-in curry-cut, plain yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, red chili powder, turmeric powder, salt, mustard oil, onion, thinly sliced, whole spices (bay leaf, cinnamon, cardamom, cloves), tomato, chopped, cumin-coriander powder, garam masala powder, hot water

What it should actually look like: Bone-in mutton in a thick, glossy dark brown to near-black reduced onion-yogurt masala clinging to the meat (not a thin gravy).
