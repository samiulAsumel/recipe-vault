Galouti Kebab (گلوٹی کباب)
India - Snacks

An extremely tender, finely minced mutton kebab from Lucknow, blended with raw papaya and warming Awadhi spices, and shallow-fried in ghee until deeply browned — famous for its melt-in-the-mouth texture.

Key ingredients: minced mutton, raw papaya, ginger-garlic paste, fried onion, roasted gram flour, cashew paste, galouti masala, ghee

What it should actually look like: Small, thin, dark brown round patties with a lightly crisp, seared surface, plated on or beside a thin paratha with sliced onion rings and mint chutney — the patties should look completely smooth-surfaced, not chunky or textured like a typical kofta.

Suggested search terms: "Lucknowi galouti kebab", "Galouti kebab Awadhi", "Tunday kababi style kebab"
