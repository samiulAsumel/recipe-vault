Mughlai Paratha (মোগলাই পরোটা)
India - Snacks

A Kolkata street food classic — a flatbread stuffed with spiced minced meat and a custardy egg layer, folded into a packet and shallow-fried until crisp and golden.

Key ingredients: all-purpose flour, minced mutton or chicken, onion, green chilies, garam masala, eggs, oil/ghee

What it should actually look like: A golden-brown, crisp-edged square or rectangular flatbread, sliced into strips showing a set egg layer and spiced minced meat filling inside — distinct from a plain paratha by its thick, stuffed, pillowy cross-section.

Suggested search terms: "Mughlai paratha Kolkata", "Kolkata street food paratha", "Mughlai paratha egg keema"
