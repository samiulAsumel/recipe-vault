Masala Dosa (ಮಸಾಲೆ ದೋಸೆ)
India - Mains

A thin, crisp, fermented rice-and-lentil crepe filled with lightly spiced mashed potato — South India's most iconic breakfast dish.

Key ingredients: raw rice (idli/dosa rice), urad dal, fenugreek seeds, potatoes, oil, mustard seeds, curry leaves, green chilies, onion, turmeric powder

What it should actually look like: A large, thin, golden-brown crepe folded over (often into a cone or half-moon shape) with visible mashed potato filling at the open end, usually served with small bowls of white coconut chutney and orange/yellow sambar.

Suggested search terms: "Masala dosa", "Dosa with potato filling", "South Indian dosa"
