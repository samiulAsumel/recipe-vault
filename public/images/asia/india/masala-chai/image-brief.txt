Masala Chai (मसाला चाय)
India - Drinks

Strong black tea simmered with milk, sugar, and a warming blend of spices like ginger, cardamom, and cloves — India's most iconic everyday drink, sold from millions of roadside stalls nationwide.

Key ingredients: black tea, whole milk, ginger, cardamom, cloves, cinnamon, sugar

What it should actually look like: A small glass or clay cup filled with warm caramel-brown milky tea, often shown steaming, sometimes with a few whole spices (cinnamon stick, cardamom pods) visible nearby or a kettle/pot in the background suggesting a roadside stall setting.
