Upma (உப்புமா)
India - Mains

A savory South Indian breakfast of roasted semolina cooked with tempered mustard seeds, curry leaves, and vegetables into a soft, fluffy dish.

Key ingredients: semolina (rava), mustard seeds, urad dal, curry leaves, onion, carrot, green peas, cashews

What it should actually look like: A plate of soft, pale golden, porridge-like semolina with visible small vegetable pieces (carrot, peas) and whole cashews, topped with fresh coriander — moist and fluffy, not dry or crumbly, and not a thick paste.

Suggested search terms: "South Indian upma", "Rava upma breakfast", "Vegetable upma recipe"
