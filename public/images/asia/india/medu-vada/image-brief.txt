Medu Vada (மெது வடை)
India - Snacks

A whipped, seasoned urad dal batter shaped into a ring and deep-fried until crisp outside and fluffy inside — a beloved South Indian savory fritter.

Key ingredients: urad dal, green chilies, ginger, curry leaves, onion, black peppercorns, cumin seeds, oil for deep frying

What it should actually look like: A stack or plate of golden-brown, ring-shaped (donut-like) fried fritters with a visible hole in the center, often served with small bowls of coconut chutney and sambar.

Suggested search terms: "Medu vada", "Vada South Indian", "Urad dal vada"
