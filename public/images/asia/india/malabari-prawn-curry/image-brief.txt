Malabari Prawn Curry (മലബാർ ചെമ്മീൻ കറി)
India - Mains

A coconut milk-based Kerala curry of prawns cooked with shallots, curry leaves, and the tangy souring agent kudampuli — a signature Malabar Coast seafood dish.

Key ingredients: prawns, coconut oil, shallots, curry leaves, kudampuli (Malabar tamarind), coconut milk, kashmiri red chili powder

What it should actually look like: A bowl of prawns in a glossy, medium-thick reddish-brown gravy with a creamy sheen from coconut milk, curry leaves and shallot pieces visible throughout — distinct from Kerala Fish Curry by using prawns and having a slightly darker, tangier gravy from kudampuli.

Suggested search terms: "Malabar prawn curry", "Kerala chemmeen curry", "Malabari style prawn curry"
