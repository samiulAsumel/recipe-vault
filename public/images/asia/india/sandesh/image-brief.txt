Sandesh (সন্দেশ)
India - Desserts

A delicate, lightly sweetened Bengali confection made from fresh paneer-like chenna, gently cooked and shaped into simple forms — prized for showcasing the clean, milky flavor of the chenna itself.

Key ingredients: fresh chenna (curdled milk), sugar, cardamom, saffron, pistachio garnish

What it should actually look like: A few small, pale off-white to light cream-colored discs or molded shapes with a smooth, slightly matte surface, sometimes bearing a decorative pressed pattern, garnished with a few pistachio slivers or saffron strands, arranged on a small plate.

Suggested search terms: "Sandesh Bengali sweet", "Bengali chenna sweet", "Sondesh mishti"
