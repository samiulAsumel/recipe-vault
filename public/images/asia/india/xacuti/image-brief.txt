Xacuti (शाकुती)
India - Mains

A Goan curry built on a dark, dry-roasted coconut-and-spice masala, simmered with chicken into a deeply flavored, glossy gravy with layered heat.

Key ingredients: dried red chilies, grated coconut, poppy seeds, coriander seeds, whole spices, chicken, onion, tamarind

What it should actually look like: A bowl of chicken pieces in a thick, dark reddish-brown to almost black-brown glossy gravy — noticeably darker than a typical red curry, with no visible cream or yogurt, served alongside rice.

Suggested search terms: "Goan chicken xacuti", "Xacuti curry Goa", "Chicken xacuti dark curry"
