Baingan ka Bharta (बैंगन का भरता)
India - Mains

Whole eggplant roasted directly over an open flame until smoky and soft, then mashed and cooked with tomatoes, onions, and spices — a beloved smoky North Indian vegetarian favorite.

Key ingredients: eggplant (brinjal), onion, tomatoes, ginger-garlic paste, green chilies, cumin

What it should actually look like: A bowl of coarsely mashed, brownish-green eggplant mixed into a thick reddish-brown tomato-onion masala, garnished with fresh coriander — visibly chunky/textured rather than a smooth purée, with no whole pieces of eggplant skin visible.

Suggested search terms: "Baingan bharta roasted eggplant", "Smoky eggplant curry India", "Punjabi baingan bharta"
