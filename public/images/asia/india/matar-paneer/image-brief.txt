Matar Paneer (मटर पनीर)
India - Mains

A comforting North Indian curry of green peas and soft paneer cubes simmered in a mild tomato-onion gravy — a staple of everyday home cooking.

Key ingredients: paneer, green peas, tomatoes, onion, ginger-garlic paste, cumin, garam masala

What it should actually look like: A bowl of soft white paneer cubes and bright green peas in a smooth, medium-thick reddish-orange gravy, garnished with fresh coriander — a simpler, less richly colored gravy than paneer butter masala, with no cream visible.

Suggested search terms: "Matar paneer curry", "Peas paneer gravy", "North Indian matar paneer"
