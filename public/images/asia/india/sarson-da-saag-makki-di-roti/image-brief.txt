Sarson da Saag with Makki di Roti (ਸਰੋਂ ਦਾ ਸਾਗ ਤੇ ਮੱਕੀ ਦੀ ਰੋਟੀ)
India - Mains

Slow-cooked mustard and spinach greens mashed into a thick, mellow dish, served with cornmeal flatbread and a generous topping of butter — Punjab's iconic winter meal.

Key ingredients: mustard greens, spinach, bathua, ginger, garlic, makki atta (cornmeal), ghee, butter, onion, tomato

What it should actually look like: A bowl of thick, deep green mashed saag with a visible pat of white/yellow butter melting on top, served alongside pale yellow, slightly thick round cornmeal flatbreads (not thin like wheat roti) with light char spots.

Suggested search terms: "Sarson da saag", "Makki di roti", "Punjabi mustard greens"
