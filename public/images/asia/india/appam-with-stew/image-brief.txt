Appam with Stew (അപ്പം)
India - Mains

A fermented rice-and-coconut batter cooked into a lacy-edged, soft-centered pancake, served with a mild coconut milk chicken stew — a beloved Kerala Christian breakfast.

Key ingredients: raw rice, cooked rice, grated coconut, yeast, chicken, coconut oil, onion, curry leaves, whole spices, potatoes, thin and thick coconut milk

What it should actually look like: A bowl-shaped pancake with thin, lacy, golden-brown crisp edges and a soft, white, spongy center, served beside a bowl of pale golden, mildly spiced chicken-and-potato stew with a creamy coconut milk gravy.
