Rajma Chawal (राजमा चावल)
India - Mains

Red kidney beans slow-simmered in a spiced onion-tomato gravy, served over steamed rice — a North Indian Sunday lunch favorite.

Key ingredients: red kidney beans (rajma), oil, cumin seeds, onion, ginger-garlic paste, tomato puree, red chili powder, turmeric, coriander powder, garam masala, basmati rice

What it should actually look like: A plate or bowl showing a mound of white steamed rice with a thick, reddish-brown kidney bean curry ladled generously over or beside it, whole beans visible in a clinging (not watery) gravy, often garnished with coriander leaves.

Suggested search terms: "Rajma chawal", "Kidney bean curry rice", "Rajma rice"
