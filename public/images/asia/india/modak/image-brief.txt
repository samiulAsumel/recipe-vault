Modak (मोदक)
India - Desserts

Delicate steamed dumplings of rice flour dough, pleated by hand into a distinctive shape and filled with sweetened fresh coconut and jaggery — Maharashtra's beloved Ganesh Chaturthi offering.

Key ingredients: rice flour dough, fresh grated coconut, jaggery, cardamom

What it should actually look like: Small, pale white, cone-shaped dumplings with a distinctive pleated, ridged top coming to a point, slightly translucent from steaming, arranged on a plate — a few may be plain white, since modak is not fried or browned.

Suggested search terms: "Modak Ganesh Chaturthi sweet", "Ukadiche modak steamed", "Modak coconut jaggery dumpling"
