Dhokla (ઢોકળા)
India - Snacks

A steamed, spongy gram-flour cake finished with a tempered mustard-seed syrup — a beloved Gujarati snack, light and tangy.

Key ingredients: gram flour (besan), semolina, yogurt, turmeric, fruit salt (eno), mustard seeds, curry leaves, green chilies, sesame seeds, coriander leaves

What it should actually look like: Small pale-yellow spongy squares with a visibly airy, perforated interior, scattered with black mustard seeds and curry leaves on top, often garnished with coriander and grated coconut.

Suggested search terms: "Dhokla", "Gujarati dhokla", "Steamed gram flour cake"
