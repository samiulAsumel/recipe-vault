Misal Pav (मिसळ पाव)
India - Mains

A fiery Maharashtrian curry of sprouted moth beans in a spiced, tempered gravy, topped with crunchy farsan and served with soft pav bread — one of Maharashtra's most celebrated breakfasts.

Key ingredients: sprouted moth beans, spiced red gravy, farsan (crunchy chickpea-flour mix), onion, pav bread, lemon

What it should actually look like: A deep bowl of thick, reddish-orange lentil gravy with visible oil sheen, generously topped with a mound of crisp golden farsan and chopped raw onion, a lemon wedge and a couple of soft bread rolls on the side.

Suggested search terms: "Misal pav", "Kolhapuri misal", "Maharashtrian misal pav breakfast"
