Vada Pav (वडा पाव)
India - Snacks

Mumbai's iconic "Indian burger" — a spiced, deep-fried potato fritter (vada) stuffed into a soft bread roll (pav) with garlic chutney and fried green chilies.

Key ingredients: potato, chickpea flour batter, pav bread roll, dry garlic chutney, green chutney, fried green chili

What it should actually look like: A round, golden-brown deep-fried potato ball wedged inside a soft, slightly squished bread bun, with a smear of reddish chutney visible at the edges, typically plated alongside a whole fried green chili and paper wrapping.

Suggested search terms: "Vada pav", "Mumbai vada pav street food", "Indian potato burger"
