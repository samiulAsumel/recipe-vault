Bharwa Baingan (भरवां बैंगन)
India - Mains

Baby eggplants slit and stuffed with a spiced peanut, sesame, and coconut masala, then slow-cooked until tender — a hearty North Indian vegetarian favorite.

Key ingredients: baby eggplants, peanuts, sesame seeds, dried coconut, coriander seeds, tamarind, jaggery

What it should actually look like: A plate of whole small eggplants, still attached to their stems, with visible coarse brown-yellow masala peeking from the slits, sitting in a light, thick sauce — the eggplants should look fully softened but intact, not mashed or falling apart.
