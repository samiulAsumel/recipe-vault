Jalebi (जलेबी)
India - Desserts

Crisp, deep-fried spirals of fermented batter soaked in warm sugar syrup — a glossy, tangy-sweet Indian classic sold hot from halwai shops and street stalls across the country.

Key ingredients: fermented flour batter, sugar syrup, saffron, cardamom, ghee or oil for frying

What it should actually look like: A tangled pile of bright orange, glossy, spiral-shaped fried sweets with a crisp, syrup-soaked surface, often photographed hot and glistening, sometimes with a small bowl of rabri (thickened milk) alongside.

Suggested search terms: "Jalebi Indian sweet", "Jalebi hot syrup spiral", "Jalebi with rabri"
