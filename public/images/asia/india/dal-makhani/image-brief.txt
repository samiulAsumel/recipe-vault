Dal Makhani (दाल मखनी)
India - Sides

Whole black lentils and kidney beans slow-simmered for hours into a rich, creamy dal finished with butter and cream — a North Indian restaurant classic.

Key ingredients: whole black lentils (sabut urad dal), red kidney beans (rajma), butter, onion, ginger-garlic paste, tomato puree, red chili powder, cumin powder, garam masala, heavy cream, kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves)

What it should actually look like: A bowl or kadai of thick, creamy, deep-brown dal with a visible swirl of cream and a small pat of butter melting on top — smooth and glossy, not thin or watery.

Suggested search terms: "Dal makhani", "Black dal", "Dal makhani creamy"
