Litti Chokha (लिट्टी चोखा)
India - Mains

Whole-wheat dough balls stuffed with spiced roasted gram flour (sattu), traditionally roasted over embers, served dunked in ghee alongside smoky mashed eggplant and potato chokha — a rustic, deeply satisfying Bihari classic.

Key ingredients: whole wheat flour, sattu (roasted gram flour), mustard oil, roasted eggplant, potato, tomato, ghee

What it should actually look like: A few round, roughly textured, golden-brown to lightly charred dough balls glistening with ghee, plated next to a coarse, olive-green-brown mashed vegetable chokha flecked with visible char and chopped onion, often on a rustic metal or banana-leaf plate.

Suggested search terms: "Litti chokha", "Bihari litti chokha", "Sattu litti with baingan chokha"
