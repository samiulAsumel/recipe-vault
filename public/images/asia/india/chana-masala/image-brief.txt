Chana Masala (छोले मसाला)
India - Mains

Chickpeas simmered in a tangy, spiced tomato-onion gravy with a distinctive dark color from chana masala spice blend — a North Indian vegetarian staple.

Key ingredients: chickpeas, tomatoes, onion, ginger-garlic paste, chana masala powder, amchur (dried mango powder)

What it should actually look like: A bowl of dark brown, whole chickpeas in a thick, glossy reddish-brown gravy, garnished with fresh coriander, raw onion rings, and a lemon wedge — the chickpeas and gravy should share a similar deep brown tone, not bright orange or red.

Suggested search terms: "Chana masala chickpea curry", "Chole curry North Indian", "Punjabi chana masala"
