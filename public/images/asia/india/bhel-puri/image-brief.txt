Bhel Puri (भेल पुरी)
India - Snacks

A crunchy, tangy Mumbai street-food mix of puffed rice, crisp sev, boiled potato, raw vegetables, and two contrasting chutneys, tossed together fresh right before serving.

Key ingredients: puffed rice, sev, boiled potato, onion, tomato, mint-coriander chutney, tamarind-jaggery chutney

What it should actually look like: A bowl or paper cone piled with pale puffed rice mixed with thin golden sev, flecks of chopped red tomato and onion, drizzled with green and dark tamarind chutney, topped with fresh coriander — a loose, textured mix, not a smooth dish.

Suggested search terms: "Bhel puri", "Mumbai bhel puri street food", "Indian puffed rice chaat"
