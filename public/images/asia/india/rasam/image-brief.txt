Rasam (ரசம்)
India - Soups

A thin, tangy, peppery South Indian soup of tamarind, tomato, and spices, tempered with mustard seeds and curry leaves — a comforting staple of the South Indian meal.

Key ingredients: tamarind, tomatoes, black pepper, cumin, garlic, rasam powder, toor dal, mustard seeds, curry leaves, dried red chilies

What it should actually look like: A small bowl or cup of thin, watery, deep reddish-orange broth with a light froth on top, flecks of curry leaves and mustard seeds visible, garnished with fresh coriander leaves — not thick or stew-like.

Suggested search terms: "South Indian rasam", "Tamil rasam soup", "Rasam tomato pepper soup"
