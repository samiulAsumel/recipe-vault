Bisi Bele Bath (ಬಿಸಿ ಬೇಳೆ ಬಾತ್)
India - Mains

Rice, lentils, and vegetables cooked together with a roasted spice powder and a ghee tempering — a hearty, complete Karnataka one-pot meal.

Key ingredients: rice, toor dal, coriander seeds, chana dal, urad dal, dried red chilies, grated coconut, mixed vegetables, tamarind, mustard seeds, curry leaves, roasted peanuts

What it should actually look like: A bowl of thick, porridge-like, reddish-brown rice-and-lentil mixture with visible chunks of vegetables throughout, topped with a scatter of curry leaves and roasted peanuts/cashews — no separate rice grains, a mushy khichdi-like consistency, not a dry pilaf.

Suggested search terms: "Bisi bele bath", "Karnataka rice dish", "Bisibelebath"
