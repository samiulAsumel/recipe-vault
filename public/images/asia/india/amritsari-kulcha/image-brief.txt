Amritsari Kulcha (ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤਸਰੀ ਕੁਲਚਾ)
India - Breads

A leavened flour dough stuffed with spiced potato, cooked in a tandoor until charred and puffed — Amritsar's iconic street food bread.

Key ingredients: all-purpose flour (maida), yogurt, baking powder, potatoes, green chilies, ginger, coriander leaves, ajwain, chaat masala, butter, nigella/sesame seeds

What it should actually look like: A thick, oval or round flatbread with a golden-brown surface, visible charred/blackened spots, and a glossy sheen from butter, often with a scatter of black nigella or sesame seeds on top; served alongside a bowl of chole and sliced onion.

Suggested search terms: "Amritsari kulcha", "Kulcha bread", "Stuffed tandoori kulcha"
