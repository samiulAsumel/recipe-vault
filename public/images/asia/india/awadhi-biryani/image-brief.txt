Awadhi Biryani (اودھی بریانی)
India - Mains

A Lucknowi biryani of basmati rice and mutton, each partially cooked separately then layered and slow-cooked together under a sealed dum lid — milder and more delicately fragrant than many other biryani styles.

Key ingredients: basmati rice, mutton, yogurt, saffron, kewra water, fried onions, whole garam spices

What it should actually look like: A pale golden, not deep-red, biryani with long, separate, fluffy grains lightly streaked with saffron, mutton pieces mixed throughout, and a scattering of fried onions, cashews, and raisins — visibly milder-colored than Hyderabadi biryani.

Suggested search terms: "Awadhi biryani Lucknow", "Lucknowi dum biryani", "Awadhi mutton biryani"
