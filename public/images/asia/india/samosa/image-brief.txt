Samosa (समोसा)
India - Snacks

A crisp, golden, triangular pastry filled with spiced mashed potatoes and peas, deep-fried until flaky — one of India's best-known snacks, found on street corners and at every tea stall.

Key ingredients: all-purpose flour pastry, boiled potato, green peas, garam masala, cumin, dry mango powder

What it should actually look like: A few deep golden-brown, triangular, crisp-shelled pastries, plated with small bowls of dark tamarind chutney and green mint chutney, sometimes with one samosa cut open to reveal the pale, chunky potato-pea filling.

Suggested search terms: "Samosa", "Indian samosa street food", "Aloo samosa with chutney"
