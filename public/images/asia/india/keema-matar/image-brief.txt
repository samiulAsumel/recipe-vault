Keema Matar (कीमा मटर)
India - Mains

Minced mutton or chicken cooked with green peas in a spiced tomato-onion base — a quick, comforting North Indian everyday favorite.

Key ingredients: minced mutton or chicken, green peas, onion, tomatoes, ginger-garlic paste, garam masala

What it should actually look like: A thick, mostly dry (not soupy) bowl of small, evenly browned meat crumbles mixed with bright green peas in a reddish-brown coating, garnished with coriander — no large meat chunks, and no thin watery gravy pooling at the bottom.

Suggested search terms: "Keema matar mince curry", "Keema peas North Indian", "Minced meat matar curry"
