Prawn Balchao (बालचाव)
India - Mains

A Goan dish of prawns cooked in a thick, tangy, spicy red chili-and-vinegar paste — a Portuguese-influenced pickle-style curry meant to be served in small, intense portions.

Key ingredients: prawns, dried Kashmiri red chilies, garlic, ginger, vinegar, onion, curry leaves

What it should actually look like: A small bowl of prawns coated in a thick, glossy, deep reddish-brown paste-like sauce with visible oil sheen at the edges — much thicker and darker than a typical prawn curry, closer to a chutney or relish in consistency.

Suggested search terms: "Goan prawn balchao", "Prawn balchao pickle curry", "Balchao Goa seafood"
