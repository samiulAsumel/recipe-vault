Bengali Shukto (শুক্তো)
India - Sides

A mildly bitter, mixed-vegetable dish simmered in a light milk-based gravy with mustard, poppy seed, and radhuni — the traditional opening course of a Bengali meal.

Key ingredients: bitter melon, potato, raw banana, drumstick, eggplant, flat beans, sweet potato, mustard paste, poppy seed paste, milk, ghee, radhuni

What it should actually look like: A bowl of mixed vegetable batons in a thin, pale yellow, lightly creamy gravy — visibly light and soupy rather than a thick curry, with distinct pieces of bitter melon, potato, and drumstick visible throughout.

Suggested search terms: "Bengali shukto", "West Bengal shukto", "Shukto mixed vegetable curry"
