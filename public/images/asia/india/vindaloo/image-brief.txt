Vindaloo (विंदालू)
India - Mains

Pork marinated in a sharp vinegar-garlic-chili paste and slow-simmered until tender — Goa's most famous Indo-Portuguese dish.

Key ingredients: dried kashmiri red chilies, garlic, ginger, cumin seeds, black peppercorns, mustard seeds, cinnamon, cloves, malt/cane vinegar, pork shoulder, onion

What it should actually look like: A bowl or kadai of dark reddish-brown pork curry, thick sauce clinging to bone-in or cubed meat pieces, visibly darker/redder than a typical North Indian curry due to the chili-vinegar paste.

Suggested search terms: "Goan vindaloo", "Pork vindaloo", "Vindaloo curry"
