Chole Bhature (छोले भटूरे)
India - Mains

A tangy, spiced chickpea curry served with hot, puffed, deep-fried bread — a beloved North Indian breakfast and street food combination.

Key ingredients: white chickpeas (kabuli chana), tea (for color), oil, onion, ginger-garlic paste, tomatoes, chole masala powder, red chili powder, anardana/amchur, all-purpose flour (maida), semolina, yogurt, baking powder

What it should actually look like: A plate showing a bowl of deep-brown, thick chickpea curry next to one or two large puffed, golden-brown fried breads (noticeably domed/inflated, not flat), often with sliced onion and a lime wedge on the side.

Suggested search terms: "Chole bhature", "Chana bhatura", "Punjabi chole bhature"
