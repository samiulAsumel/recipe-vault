Bebinca (बेबिंका)
India - Desserts

A rich, dense Goan layered cake made by baking thin coconut-milk batter layers one at a time until golden, resulting in a distinctive striped, chewy-yet-tender dessert with Portuguese colonial roots.

Key ingredients: coconut milk, all-purpose flour, egg yolks, ghee, nutmeg

What it should actually look like: A wedge or round slice of cake clearly showing many thin, distinct golden-brown and pale striped layers stacked on top of each other, with a dense, glossy, slightly caramelized surface.
