Sabudana Khichdi (साबुदाणा खिचडी)
India - Mains

A Maharashtrian dish of soaked tapioca pearls tempered with cumin, peanuts, and potato — a light, popular choice during religious fasting periods.

Key ingredients: sabudana (tapioca pearls), potato, peanuts, cumin seeds, curry leaves, green chilies, ghee

What it should actually look like: A plate of small, translucent, glossy white-to-clear pearls with visible diced potato and crushed peanuts throughout, garnished with fresh coriander — the pearls should look separate and slightly shiny, not a sticky, clumped mass.

Suggested search terms: "Sabudana khichdi", "Sago khichdi fasting food", "Maharashtrian sabudana"
