Kerala Fish Curry (കേരള മീൻ കറി)
India - Mains

Fish simmered in a tangy, coconut milk and kokum gravy — a defining dish of Kerala's coastal cooking tradition.

Key ingredients: fish steaks (kingfish/pomfret), coconut oil, mustard seeds, shallots, curry leaves, kashmiri red chili powder, kokum, coconut milk

What it should actually look like: A bowl or clay pot of fish pieces in a thick, deep reddish-brown gravy with a slightly creamy sheen from coconut milk, curry leaves visible throughout, often served alongside plain white rice.

Suggested search terms: "Kerala fish curry", "Meen curry", "Kerala meen curry kokum"
