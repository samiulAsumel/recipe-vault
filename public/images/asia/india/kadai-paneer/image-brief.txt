Kadai Paneer (कड़ाई पनीर)
India - Mains

A North Indian dish of paneer and bell peppers cooked in a coarsely ground, freshly roasted spice masala with tomatoes — a bold, restaurant-style favorite.

Key ingredients: paneer, bell peppers (capsicum), coriander seeds, dried red chilies, tomatoes, onion, kasuri methi

What it should actually look like: A thick, coarse-textured, reddish-orange tomato masala coating cubes of white paneer and colorful bell pepper pieces (red, yellow, green), garnished with julienned ginger — visibly chunkier and drier than the smoother, creamier paneer butter masala.

Suggested search terms: "Kadai paneer restaurant style", "Kadhai paneer capsicum curry", "North Indian kadai paneer"
