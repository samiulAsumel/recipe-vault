Kashmiri Pulao (کشمیری پلاؤ)
India - Mains

A mildly sweet, fragrant basmati rice dish from Kashmir, studded with dried fruits, nuts, and paneer, and gently perfumed with saffron and whole spices.

Key ingredients: basmati rice, saffron, milk, paneer, cashews, almonds, raisins, dried apricots, pomegranate seeds

What it should actually look like: A plate of pale golden-yellow, fluffy, separate rice grains dotted with visible golden-fried paneer cubes, nuts, raisins, and bright red pomegranate seeds on top — visibly sweeter and more colorful than a typical savory pulao, with no thick gravy or sauce.

Suggested search terms: "Kashmiri pulao rice", "Kashmiri fruit nut pulao", "Kashmiri pulao paneer"
