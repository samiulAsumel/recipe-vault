Filter Coffee (ஃபில்டர் காபி)
India - Drinks

A strong, aromatic coffee decoction slow-dripped through a traditional metal filter, mixed with hot milk and sugar, and frothed by pouring between two vessels — South India's iconic morning ritual.

Key ingredients: filter coffee powder (with chicory), whole milk, sugar

What it should actually look like: A traditional stainless-steel tumbler sitting inside a wider bowl (dabarah), filled with frothy, caramel-brown coffee, sometimes shown mid-pour between the two vessels to capture the characteristic froth and motion.
