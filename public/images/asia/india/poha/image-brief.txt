Poha (पोहा)
India - Mains

A light, fluffy Indian breakfast of flattened rice tempered with mustard seeds, peanuts, turmeric, and onions, finished with lemon juice and fresh coriander.

Key ingredients: flattened rice (poha), mustard seeds, curry leaves, peanuts, onion, turmeric, lemon juice, sev

What it should actually look like: A plate of pale yellow, light, fluffy flattened rice flakes with visible whole peanuts, flecks of green chili and curry leaf, topped with fresh coriander and a light scatter of crunchy sev — dry and separate-grained, never wet or clumped.

Suggested search terms: "Poha breakfast", "Indori poha", "Maharashtrian poha recipe"
