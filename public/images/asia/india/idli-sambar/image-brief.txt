Idli Sambar (இட்லி சாம்பார்)
India - Mains

Soft, steamed, fermented rice-and-lentil cakes served with a tangy, mildly spiced lentil-vegetable stew — a South Indian breakfast staple.

Key ingredients: idli/dosa rice, urad dal, fenugreek seeds, toor dal, mixed vegetables (drumstick, carrot, pumpkin, eggplant), tamarind, sambar powder, mustard seeds, curry leaves

What it should actually look like: Two or three small, round, white, soft steamed cakes on a plate next to a bowl of orange-yellow sambar with visible vegetable pieces, often with a smaller bowl of white coconut chutney alongside.

Suggested search terms: "Idli sambar", "Idli with sambar", "South Indian idli"
