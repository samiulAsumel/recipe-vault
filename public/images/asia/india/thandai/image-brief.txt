Thandai (ठंडाई)
India - Drinks

A rich, chilled North Indian milk drink blended with a fragrant paste of almonds, pistachios, melon seeds, fennel, and warming spices — a beloved Holi and Maha Shivratri specialty.

Key ingredients: milk, almonds, pistachios, melon seeds, fennel, saffron, dried rose petals

What it should actually look like: A tall glass of pale, creamy off-white to light golden liquid over ice, garnished with dried rose petals floating on top and a light dusting of ground pistachio, often with a festive, colorful backdrop suggesting Holi.

Suggested search terms: "Thandai drink glass", "Holi thandai milk", "Indian nut milk spiced drink"
