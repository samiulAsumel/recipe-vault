Paneer Tikka (पनीर टिक्का)
India - Snacks

Cubes of paneer and vegetables marinated in spiced yogurt, skewered and grilled until charred at the edges — a beloved vegetarian tandoori appetizer.

Key ingredients: paneer, thick yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, kashmiri red chili powder, garam masala, roasted gram flour (besan), bell peppers, onion, chaat masala

What it should actually look like: Skewers of orange-red marinated paneer cubes alternating with bell pepper and onion pieces, visibly charred/blackened at the edges, usually served on a sizzling platter or plate with lemon wedges and mint chutney.

Suggested search terms: "Paneer tikka", "Grilled paneer skewers", "Tandoori paneer"
