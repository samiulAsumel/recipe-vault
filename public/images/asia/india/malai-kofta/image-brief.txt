Malai Kofta (मलाई कोफ्ता)
India - Mains

Fried paneer-and-potato dumplings served in a rich, creamy cashew-tomato gravy — a beloved vegetarian North Indian restaurant classic.

Key ingredients: paneer, potatoes, cornflour, cashews, onion, tomatoes, ginger-garlic paste, butter, garam masala, heavy cream

What it should actually look like: A bowl of round, golden-fried dumplings (koftas) sitting in a smooth, glossy orange-red creamy gravy, garnished with a swirl of cream and coriander leaves on top — the koftas should look intact, not mushy or dissolved.

Suggested search terms: "Malai kofta", "Kofta curry", "Paneer kofta gravy"
