Hyderabadi Biryani (حیدرآبادی بریانی)
India - Mains

Marinated chicken and fragrant basmati rice layered and sealed with saffron, fried onions, and mint, then slow-steamed together — Hyderabad's most famous dish.

Key ingredients: chicken, plain yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, red chili powder, turmeric powder, biryani masala, lemon juice, salt, fried onions (birista), basmati rice, whole spices (bay leaf, cinnamon, cardamom, cloves, star anise), mint leaves, coriander leaves, ghee, saffron, milk, rose water

What it should actually look like: A pot or platter of layered rice showing streaks of white rice, golden saffron-tinted rice, and chicken/gravy, garnished with fried onions and fresh mint/coriander — distinct layers, not a uniformly mixed pilaf.

Suggested search terms: "Hyderabadi biryani", "Chicken dum biryani", "Biryani"
