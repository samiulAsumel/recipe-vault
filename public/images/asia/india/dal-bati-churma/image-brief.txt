Dal Bati Churma (दाल बाटी चूरमा)
India - Mains

A three-part Rajasthani classic: firm baked wheat dough balls (bati), a spiced lentil dal, and a sweet crumbled wheat-jaggery dessert (churma), all traditionally eaten together.

Key ingredients: mixed lentils, ghee, cumin, garlic, onion, tomato, whole wheat flour, semolina, ajwain, jaggery, cardamom

What it should actually look like: A thali-style plate showing round, golden-brown, slightly cracked baked bread balls (bati, often split open with dal spooned in), a bowl of thick yellow-brown dal, and a small mound of golden, crumbly sweet churma — three visually distinct components on one plate.

Suggested search terms: "Dal bati churma", "Rajasthani thali", "Baati with dal"
