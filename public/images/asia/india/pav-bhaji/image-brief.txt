Pav Bhaji (पाव भाजी)
India - Mains

A rich, buttery mash of mixed vegetables cooked with pav bhaji masala, served with soft, butter-toasted bread rolls — one of Mumbai's most beloved comfort foods.

Key ingredients: potato, cauliflower, peas, capsicum, tomato, pav bhaji masala, butter, pav bread

What it should actually look like: A thick, smooth, deep reddish-orange vegetable mash in a bowl or on a flat plate, topped with a melting pat of butter and a scatter of chopped onion and coriander, with two or more butter-toasted, lightly golden bread rolls alongside.

Suggested search terms: "Pav bhaji", "Mumbai pav bhaji street food", "Indian vegetable mash with bread"
