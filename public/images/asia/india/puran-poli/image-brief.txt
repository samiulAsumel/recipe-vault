Puran Poli (पुरण पोळी)
India - Desserts

A sweetened lentil-jaggery filling stuffed inside a thin wheat flatbread, cooked with ghee until golden — a beloved Maharashtrian and Gujarati festive sweet.

Key ingredients: chana dal, jaggery, cardamom, whole wheat flour, all-purpose flour, turmeric, ghee

What it should actually look like: Round, flat, golden-brown flatbreads with scattered darker browned spots, often stacked on a plate with a small bowl of ghee or mango pulp (aamras) alongside; no visible filling on the outside, just a slightly bumpy round bread.

Suggested search terms: "Puran poli", "Maharashtrian puran poli", "Sweet stuffed flatbread India"
