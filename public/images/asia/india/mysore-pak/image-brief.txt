Mysore Pak (ಮೈಸೂರು ಪಾಕ್)
India - Desserts

A rich, melt-in-the-mouth South Indian sweet made from gram flour, ghee, and sugar, cooked together into a golden, porous fudge — a signature specialty of Mysore, Karnataka.

Key ingredients: gram flour (besan), ghee, sugar

What it should actually look like: Neat golden-yellow squares or rectangles with a visibly porous, honeycomb-like interior texture, stacked or arranged on a plate, with a glossy sheen from the ghee.

Suggested search terms: "Mysore pak sweet", "Mysore pak besan ghee fudge", "Mysore pak squares"
