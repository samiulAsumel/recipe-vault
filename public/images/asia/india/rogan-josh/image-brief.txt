Rogan Josh (روغن جوش)
India - Mains

Tender lamb slow-simmered in a fragrant, deep-red Kashmiri chili and yogurt gravy — a signature dish of the Kashmiri Wazwan feast.

Key ingredients: lamb (bone-in curry-cut), plain yogurt, kashmiri red chili powder, salt, mustard oil, whole spices (bay leaf, black cardamom, green cardamom, cinnamon, cloves), onion, ginger-garlic paste, fennel powder, dried ginger powder (saunth), water

What it should actually look like: A bowl or kadai of bone-in lamb pieces in a thick, glossy, deep brick-red gravy, oil visibly separating at the edges — the color should look rich red, not orange like butter chicken.

Suggested search terms: "Rogan josh", "Kashmiri lamb curry", "Rogan josh lamb"
