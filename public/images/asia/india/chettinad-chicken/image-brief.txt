Chettinad Chicken (செட்டிநாடு கோழி)
India - Mains

Chicken cooked in a freshly roasted-and-ground Chettinad spice masala of whole chilies, fennel, and coconut — a bold, aromatic South Indian classic.

Key ingredients: chicken, dried red chilies, coriander seeds, fennel seeds, black peppercorns, star anise, cinnamon, cloves, grated coconut, curry leaves, onion, tomato

What it should actually look like: A bowl or kadai of chicken pieces coated in a thick, dark brownish-red, coarse-textured masala (visibly speckled/grainy from the ground whole spices, not a smooth gravy), garnished with curry leaves and coriander.

Suggested search terms: "Chettinad chicken", "Chettinad chicken curry", "Tamil Nadu spicy chicken"
