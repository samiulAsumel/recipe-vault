Lassi (लस्सी)
India - Drinks

A cooling, blended yogurt drink from Punjab, whipped smooth and frothy with water or milk, sweetened or salted, and often topped with a spoonful of cream — India's classic hot-weather refresher.

Key ingredients: thick yogurt, water or milk, sugar, cardamom, malai (cream) topping

What it should actually look like: A tall glass or traditional clay cup filled with a thick, pale creamy-white beverage with a visible frothy top layer, topped with a dollop of cream and a few chopped pistachios.

Suggested search terms: "Lassi drink glass", "Punjabi lassi yogurt drink", "Amritsari lassi cream"
