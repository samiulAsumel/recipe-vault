Pani Puri (पानी पुरी)
India - Snacks

Crisp, hollow semolina puris filled with spiced potato and chickpeas, then dunked in tangy-spicy mint-tamarind water and eaten in one bite — India's most iconic street-food snack.

Key ingredients: semolina puris, tamarind-mint water, boiled potato, chickpeas, chaat masala, black salt

What it should actually look like: A plate of small round hollow puris, some intact and golden-brown, one or two cracked open showing potato-chickpea filling, often shown mid-dunk in a bowl of green-tinted spiced water, with a vendor's tray or steel plate in the background.

Suggested search terms: "Pani puri", "Golgappa", "Puchka street food India"
