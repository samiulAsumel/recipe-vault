Aloo Paratha (आलू पराठा)
India - Breads

A whole wheat flatbread stuffed with spiced mashed potato and shallow-fried in ghee until crisp and golden — a beloved Punjabi breakfast staple.

Key ingredients: whole wheat flour, potatoes, green chilies, ginger, cumin, amchur, garam masala, ghee

What it should actually look like: A round, golden-brown flatbread with visible browned crisp spots on the surface, torn open to reveal a soft, pale yellow spiced potato filling inside, often topped with a melting pat of butter.

Suggested search terms: "Aloo paratha stuffed flatbread", "Punjabi aloo paratha", "Indian potato stuffed bread"
