Laal Maas (लाल मांस)
India - Mains

Mutton slow-cooked in a fiery, deep red Rajasthani chili and yogurt gravy — one of India's spiciest traditional curries, born from Rajput royal hunting feasts.

Key ingredients: mutton, plain yogurt, dried mathania/kashmiri red chilies, garlic, ginger, ghee or mustard oil, onion, whole spices, coriander powder

What it should actually look like: A bowl or kadai of bone-in mutton pieces in a vividly deep red, glossy gravy — noticeably more intensely red than a typical curry (like rogan josh but even deeper/darker red), with visible oil separation at the edges.

Suggested search terms: "Laal maas", "Rajasthani mutton curry", "Red mutton curry Rajasthan"
