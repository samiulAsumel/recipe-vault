Chicken Chettinad, Dry Roast (செட்டிநாடு கோழி வறுவல்)
India - Mains

Chicken marinated and cooked dry with roasted whole spices, curry leaves, and coconut slivers until richly coated and nearly free of gravy — the varuval-style sibling of Chettinad chicken curry.

Key ingredients: chicken, dried red chilies, coriander seeds, fennel seeds, shallots, curry leaves, coconut, coconut oil

What it should actually look like: A plate or bowl of dark, richly browned chicken pieces thickly coated in a dry, coarse dark-brown masala with visible curry leaves and fried coconut slivers scattered on top — no pooled sauce or gravy visible, distinctly drier and darker than the existing gravy-based Chettinad Chicken curry.

Suggested search terms: "Chettinad chicken varuval", "Dry roast chicken Chettinad", "Chettinad chicken fry no gravy"
