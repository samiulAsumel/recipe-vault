Dhansak (ધનસાક)
India - Mains

A Parsi dish of lentils, vegetables, and mutton cooked together and blended into a thick, hearty stew with a distinctive sweet-sour-spicy flavor, traditionally served with caramelized brown rice.

Key ingredients: toor dal, masoor dal, chana dal, pumpkin, eggplant, mutton, tamarind, jaggery, dhansak masala

What it should actually look like: A bowl of thick, smooth, brownish stew (no visible chunks of lentils or vegetables, fully blended) with tender bone-in mutton pieces sitting in it, typically plated beside a mound of caramelized brown rice — distinct from a typical dal by its meat pieces and darker, richer color.

Suggested search terms: "Parsi dhansak", "Dhansak mutton lentil stew", "Dhansak rice Parsi"
