Gulab Jamun (गुलाब जामुन)
India - Desserts

Soft, deep-fried milk-solid dumplings soaked in a fragrant, rose- and cardamom-scented sugar syrup — one of India's most beloved and widely recognized sweets.

Key ingredients: khoya (milk solids), sugar syrup, cardamom, saffron, rose water

What it should actually look like: Several dark reddish-brown, glossy, round dumplings sitting in a bowl of light amber syrup, garnished with a few strands of saffron and chopped pistachios, with a soft, spongy texture visible where one might be cut open.

Suggested search terms: "Gulab jamun", "Indian milk dumpling sweet in syrup", "Gulab jamun dessert bowl"
