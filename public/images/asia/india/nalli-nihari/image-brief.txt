Nalli Nihari (نلی نہاری)
India - Mains

A slow-simmered Old Delhi mutton shank stew with a deep, dark gravy thickened by wheat flour, traditionally served with a bone-marrow garnish.

Key ingredients: mutton shanks, onion, ginger-garlic paste, nihari masala (whole spices), wheat flour, ginger, green chilies, lime

What it should actually look like: A bowl of dark reddish-brown, glossy, medium-thick gravy with tender bone-in mutton shank pieces, topped with julienned ginger, sliced green chilies, and fresh coriander, with a lime wedge on the side — the gravy should look rich and opaque, not thin or watery.

Suggested search terms: "Nalli nihari Delhi", "Old Delhi nihari mutton", "Nihari bone marrow stew"
