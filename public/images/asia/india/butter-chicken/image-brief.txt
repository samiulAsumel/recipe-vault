Butter Chicken (मुर्ग़ मक्खनी)
India - Mains

Tender marinated chicken simmered in a velvety tomato-butter gravy finished with cream and kasuri methi — India's most internationally famous curry.

Key ingredients: chicken thighs, plain yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, kashmiri red chili powder, garam masala, lemon juice, salt, butter, onion, tomatoes, sugar, water, heavy cream, kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves)

What it should actually look like: A bowl or kadai of glossy, brick-orange-red gravy coating chicken pieces, with a visible swirl of cream on top and a scatter of kasuri methi leaves, usually served with naan or basmati rice.

Suggested search terms: "Butter chicken", "Murgh makhani", "Butter chicken curry"
