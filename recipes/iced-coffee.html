Iced Coffee

Strong coffee cooled and poured over ice with milk.

Vegetarian Gluten-Free Nut-Free
Prep5 min
Cook--
Total5 min
Servings 1 serving
DifficultyEasy

Coffee brewed double-strength, cooled, and poured over ice with milk -- a far better result than pouring hot coffee straight over ice, which waters it down as it melts.

Ingredients

  • 240 ml strong brewed coffee, cooled
  • 1 cup ice cubes
  • 60 ml milk or cream, to taste
  • 1 tbsp simple syrup, optional

Instructions

  1. Brewing it extra strong is what keeps the coffee from tasting watery once the ice starts to melt.

Notes

  • Brew a batch ahead and refrigerate it specifically for iced coffee -- pouring hot coffee straight over ice dilutes it fast.
  • Cold brew concentrate works even better if you have the time to make it.
