Chocolate Brownies

Dense, fudgy squares with a crackly top.

Vegetarian
Prep15 min
Cook30 min
Total1 hr
Servings 12 servings
DifficultyEasy

Melted chocolate and butter whisked with sugar and eggs until glossy, baked just until set -- the crackly top is the sign you got it right.

Ingredients

  • 200 g dark chocolate, chopped
  • 170 g butter
  • 250 g sugar
  • 3 eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 100 g all-purpose flour
  • 30 g cocoa powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt

To finish

  • chocolate chips, optional

Instructions

  4. Whisking hard at this stage is what gives brownies their shiny, crackly top -- do not skip it.

Notes

  • Slight underbaking is what keeps brownies fudgy -- a toothpick should come out with a few moist crumbs, not clean.
  • Keeps 4 days in an airtight container at room temperature.
