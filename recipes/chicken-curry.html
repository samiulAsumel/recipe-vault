Chicken Curry

A one-pot chicken curry with a spice base you can adjust to taste — mild for family dinners, hot if you want it.

Ingredients

  • 800g chicken thighs, cut into pieces
  • 2 onions, finely chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 2 tbsp curry powder
  • 1 can (400ml) coconut milk
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh cilantro, to garnish

Instructions

  1. Heat the oil in a large pot over medium heat and cook the onions until soft and golden, about 8 minutes.
  2. Add the garlic and ginger, and cook for another minute until fragrant.
  3. Stir in the curry powder and cook for 30 seconds to toast the spices.
  4. Add the chicken pieces and cook, stirring occasionally, until lightly browned on all sides.
  5. Pour in the coconut milk, add salt, and bring to a gentle simmer.
  6. Cover and simmer for 20–25 minutes, until the chicken is cooked through and the sauce has thickened slightly.
  7. Taste and adjust salt or spice, then garnish with fresh cilantro before serving.
