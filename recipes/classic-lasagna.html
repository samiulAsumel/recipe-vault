Classic Lasagna

Layered pasta baked with a slow-simmered meat sauce and bechamel.

Nut-Free
Prep30 min
Cook1 hr 15 min
Total2 hr
Servings 8 servings
DifficultyHard

Sheets of pasta layered with a long-simmered beef and tomato sauce and a creamy bechamel, baked until bubbling and golden on top.

Ingredients

For the meat sauce

  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 onion, finely diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 500 g ground beef
  • 800 g crushed tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • salt and black pepper to taste

For the bechamel

  • 50 g butter
  • 50 g all-purpose flour
  • 600 ml milk
  • pinch of nutmeg
  • salt to taste

To assemble

  • 12 lasagna sheets, cooked according to package directions
  • 200 g mozzarella, grated
  • 50 g Parmesan, grated

Instructions

  2. 8 min
  3. 35 min

    The longer, slower simmer is what makes a meat sauce taste developed instead of just cooked -- do not rush this step.

  7. 35 min
  8. 15 min

    Slicing straight from the oven collapses the layers -- resting lets the lasagna set.

Notes

  • Assembled, unbaked lasagna freezes well for up to 3 months -- thaw overnight before baking.
  • Leftovers keep 3 days refrigerated and reheat well covered in a low oven.
