Fresh Lemonade

Squeezed lemons balanced with sugar syrup and cold water.

Vegetarian Vegan Gluten-Free Dairy-Free Nut-Free
Prep10 min
Cook5 min
Total15 min
Servings 6 servings
DifficultyEasy

Fresh lemon juice mixed with a simple syrup and cold water -- brighter and less cloying than anything from a powdered mix or a bottle.

Ingredients

  • 200 g sugar
  • 240 ml water, for the syrup
  • 240 ml fresh lemon juice, about 6-8 lemons
  • 1 l cold water, plus ice to serve

To finish

  • lemon slices and mint, to garnish

Instructions

  1. 3 min

  3. Making a syrup instead of stirring sugar directly into cold water is what dissolves it fully -- sugar stirred into cold liquid alone often stays grainy.

Notes

  • Keeps 5 days refrigerated; the flavor is best within the first 2 days.
  • Adjust sweetness to taste -- lemons vary quite a bit in tartness.
