Hot Chocolate

Melted chocolate and milk whisked into a thick, rich drink.

Vegetarian Gluten-Free Nut-Free
Prep5 min
Cook10 min
Total15 min
Servings 2 servings
DifficultyEasy

Real chocolate melted directly into warm milk, not just cocoa powder stirred in -- the difference is a drink that's genuinely thick and rich, not just sweet.

Ingredients

  • 480 ml whole milk
  • 100 g dark chocolate, finely chopped
  • 1 tbsp sugar, or to taste
  • 1/4 tsp vanilla extract
  • pinch of salt

To finish

  • whipped cream or marshmallows, optional

Instructions

  2. Melting the chocolate off the heat, whisking constantly, keeps it from seizing or scorching against the bottom of the pan.

  3. 3 min

Notes

  • Use the best dark chocolate you have -- this recipe has so few ingredients that the chocolate's quality is the whole drink.
  • Steaming, not boiling, the milk is what keeps it from developing a skin.
