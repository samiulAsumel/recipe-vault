Banana Bread

A moist, dense loaf built on very ripe, spotty bananas.

Vegetarian
Prep15 min
Cook1 hr
Total1 hr 25 min
Servings 10 servings
DifficultyEasy

Overripe bananas mashed into a simple batter and baked into a moist loaf -- the best use for bananas too far gone to eat on their own.

Ingredients

  • 3 very ripe bananas, mashed
  • 115 g butter, melted
  • 150 g sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • pinch of salt
  • 190 g all-purpose flour

To finish

  • chopped walnuts, optional

Instructions

Notes

  • The spottier and blacker the banana peel, the sweeter and more flavorful the bread -- avoid bananas that are still yellow and firm.
  • Keeps 4 days at room temperature wrapped; freezes well for up to 3 months.
