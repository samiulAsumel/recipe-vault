Vegetable Spring Rolls

Crisp fried rolls packed with shredded vegetables.

Vegetarian Vegan Dairy-Free
Prep30 min
Cook15 min
Total45 min
Servings 6 servings
DifficultyMedium

A shredded vegetable filling wrapped tight in spring roll pastry and fried until crackling -- a party appetizer worth the extra folding.

Ingredients

  • 12 spring roll wrappers
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil, for the filling
  • 2 cups cabbage, shredded
  • 1 cup carrot, julienned
  • 1 cup bean sprouts
  • 3 spring onions, sliced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • oil for frying

To finish

  • sweet chili sauce or soy dipping sauce

Instructions

  2. 4 min

  3. A hot filling steams the wrapper and makes it tear -- let it cool before wrapping.

  5. 5 min

Notes

  • Rolls can be assembled ahead and refrigerated -- fry just before serving for maximum crunch.
  • Baking at 200C (400F) for 20 minutes, turning once, works as a lighter alternative to frying.
