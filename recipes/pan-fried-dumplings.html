Pan-Fried Dumplings

Pork and cabbage dumplings, steamed then crisped in the pan.

Dairy-Free Nut-Free
Prep40 min
Cook15 min
Total55 min
Servings 6 servings
DifficultyMedium

A juicy pork and cabbage filling folded into wrappers, steamed until cooked through, then finished in a hot pan for a crisp golden base.

Ingredients

  • 300 g ground pork
  • 2 cups cabbage, finely chopped and salted
  • 3 spring onions, finely sliced
  • 1 tbsp ginger, grated
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 24 dumpling wrappers
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil, for frying
  • 120 ml water, for steaming

To finish

  • soy sauce and chili oil, for dipping

Instructions

  1. Salting and squeezing the cabbage first keeps the filling from turning watery once it cooks.

  4. 3 min
  5. 8 min

Notes

  • Uncooked dumplings freeze well laid flat on a tray before being bagged.
  • Cook straight from frozen, adding about 2 extra minutes to the steaming step.
