Tabbouleh (Lebanese Parsley Salad)

A parsley salad with a little bulgur, not a bulgur salad with a little parsley.

Vegetarian Vegan Dairy-Free Nut-Free
Prep30 min
Cook--
Total50 min
Servings 4 servings
DifficultyMedium

Finely chopped flat-leaf parsley makes up most of the volume, with only a small amount of fine bulgur, ripe tomato, mint, and a lemon-olive oil dressing -- the reverse ratio of the bulgur-heavy versions common outside the Levant.

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp fine bulgur (#1 grade), rinsed
  • 4 cups flat-leaf parsley, finely chopped, tightly packed
  • 1/4 cups fresh mint leaves, finely chopped
  • 3 ripe tomatoes, finely diced
  • 4 green onions, finely sliced
  • 60 ml fresh lemon juice
  • 60 ml extra-virgin olive oil
  • salt and black pepper to taste

To serve

  • romaine or lettuce leaves, for scooping, optional

Instructions

  1. 15 min

    Fine (#1) bulgur only needs a soak, not cooking -- it should stay slightly chewy, not mushy, since it's a minor ingredient here, not the base.

  2. Excess water clinging to the parsley will make the finished salad soggy and dilute the dressing.

  3. A food processor tends to bruise and liquefy parsley instead of cutting it cleanly -- a sharp knife and patience give the dry, fluffy texture tabbouleh is known for.

  7. 5 min

Notes

  • Parsley should outweigh the bulgur by a wide margin -- if it reads as a grain salad with parsley sprinkled in, it isn't tabbouleh.
  • Tabbouleh is traditionally eaten by scooping with a lettuce or grape leaf rather than with a fork.
  • It keeps a day refrigerated, but the parsley wilts -- for the best texture, dress it close to serving.
