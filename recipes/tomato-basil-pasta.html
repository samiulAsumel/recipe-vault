Tomato Basil Pasta

A 30-minute weeknight pasta built on a fresh tomato sauce.

Vegetarian Nut-Free
Prep10 min
Cook25 min
Total35 min
Servings 4 servings
DifficultyEasy

Ripe tomatoes cooked down with garlic and olive oil until they collapse into a sauce, finished with torn basil and starchy pasta water. One pan, one pot, no specialty shopping.

Ingredients

  • 400 g dried spaghetti
  • salt for the pasta water

For the sauce

  • 3 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
  • 4 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
  • 800 g ripe plum tomatoes, roughly chopped
  • 1/2 tsp chilli flakes, optional
  • 30 g fresh basil, leaves torn
  • salt and black pepper to taste

To finish

  • 40 g Parmesan, finely grated

Instructions

  1. Under-salted water is the most common reason a finished pasta tastes flat.

  2. 2 min
  3. 18 min
  4. 9 min
  5. 1 min

    The starch in the water is what emulsifies the sauce -- plain water will not do it.

Notes

  • Out of season, 2 x 400g tins of good whole plum tomatoes beat fresh ones.
  • Keeps 3 days refrigerated; loosen with a splash of water when reheating.
