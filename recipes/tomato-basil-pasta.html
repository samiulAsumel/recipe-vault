Tomato Basil Pasta

A 30-minute weeknight pasta built on a simple fresh tomato sauce — no jarred sauce required.

Ingredients

  • 400g spaghetti
  • 500g ripe tomatoes, chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1/4 cup olive oil
  • 1 cup fresh basil leaves, torn
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Grated parmesan, to serve (optional)

Instructions

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil and cook the spaghetti until al dente.
  2. While the pasta cooks, heat the olive oil in a pan over medium heat and sauté the garlic until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
  3. Add the chopped tomatoes and a pinch of salt, and simmer for 10–12 minutes until the sauce thickens slightly.
  4. Drain the pasta, reserving a small cup of the pasta water.
  5. Toss the pasta into the sauce, adding a splash of pasta water if it looks dry.
  6. Stir in the torn basil off the heat, and season with salt and pepper.
  7. Serve immediately with parmesan on top, if using.
