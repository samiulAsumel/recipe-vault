Spaghetti Carbonara

Pasta tossed in a silky egg and pecorino sauce with crisp pancetta.

Nut-Free
Prep10 min
Cook15 min
Total25 min
Servings 4 servings
DifficultyMedium

Just five ingredients -- eggs, cheese, pancetta, pepper, and pasta -- combined off the heat so the eggs turn into a glossy sauce instead of scrambling.

Ingredients

  • 400 g spaghetti
  • 150 g pancetta, diced
  • 3 whole eggs
  • 2 egg yolks
  • 80 g pecorino romano, finely grated, plus extra to serve
  • plenty of black pepper
  • salt for the pasta water

Instructions

  2. 6 min

  5. The pan must be off the heat when the eggs go in -- direct heat scrambles them instead of turning them into sauce.

Notes

  • Guanciale is the traditional cut if you can find it -- pancetta is a widely available substitute.
  • Heavy cream is not part of a traditional carbonara -- the emulsified eggs are what make the sauce silky.
